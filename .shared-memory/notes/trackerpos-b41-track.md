# Batch 41 trackerpos track

## Summary
- Mobs are now drawn where the 1.7.10 client has them: `src/mob-renderer.js` takes an optional `clientOf(e)` and places each rig at `lastTickPos + (pos - lastTickPos) * partial` of the tracker's client entity (RenderManager); the light sample, the shadow list (`main.js`) and the leash/nameplate renderer (`EntityWorld.gearViews()`, views that delegate to the live mob and override x/y/z) use it too. A mob with no client entity, or one that is mounted or ridden, is drawn at its live position. The server simulation is untouched.
- `src/entity-tracker.js`: S12 velocity packets (`TrackerEntry`: motion change over 0.02 squared, or any change from a zero motion, sent before the position packet; `Entity.velocityChanged` at the end of the update; the spawn packet carries the motion; clamp 3.9, `(int)(m * 8000)`). `ClientEntity` now holds motion and the previous position, applies S12 (`/ 8000`) and spawn velocity (`(float) / 8000f`), and runs `onLivingUpdate`: 3-step interpolation, else 0.98 drag, the 0.005 cut, `moveEntityWithHeading(0, 0)` through a callback (`EntityWorld.moveClient` reuses `moveEntityWithHeading`), and the chicken's slow fall. A client whose chunk is not loaded hovers (`clientUnloaded` branch in `moveEntityWithHeading`, bytecode-read). Ghast, squid, bat, dragon, wither and blaze are interpolated only (`CLIENT_PHYSICS_OFF`).
- `knockBack` now sets `velocityChanged` (native `attackEntityFrom`), which the tracker consumes. `villager.js` already read that flag; it was never set before.

## Verification
- Executed: `tools/probes/TrackerProbe.java` now also runs a real client `EntityChicken` on a remote world double (`ahb.E` true, a chunk double answering `isChunkLoaded`) after the unchanged `my`; six scenarios in `public/research/tracker-reference.json` (`clientWalk`, `clientKnock`, `clientFall`, `clientTeleport`, `clientSpawn`, `clientUnloaded`) record the server inputs, the packets (now including `hy` and spawn velocity) and the client position, motion and previous position per tick. The packet handlers (S0F, S14, S18, S12) are replicated in `TrackerProbe.handle` from the bytecode of `NetHandlerPlayClient` (bytecode-read); the entity calls (`setPositionAndRotation2`, `setVelocity`, `onLivingUpdate`) are executed. The six batch 40 scenarios are unchanged apart from the extra spawn velocity fields.
- Not executed: `Entity.onEntityUpdate` on the client (water, fire, void checks) and the world's chunk loading.
- Focused tests: `entity-tracker` 23/23 (six new client replays at 1e-9, two velocity unit tests; a mutated 0.98 drag fails two replays), plus `entities`, `living-simulation`, `villager`, `damage-fx`, `chicken`, `horse-gear`, `mob-equipment`, `ring-events`, `dragon` together 128/128. The full suite and the build were left to the lead.

Rig root positions sit on the client segment (max distance 0.0078, the 1/128 render lift), frame steps of the walking mob: median 0.041, 95th percentile 0.087, max 0.087 blocks, so no jumps. The client trails the live position by mean 0.27 and max 0.47 block while moving. No long tasks in the last run (one 66-81 ms task showed in two earlier runs, right after the spawn, rig and shader creation), no page errors except a favicon 404. Load average 7.2 (6.9 / 5.7); not a benchmark.

## Follow-ups (proposed gap lines)
- Client movement of flying, swimming and boss mobs (ghast, squid, bat, dragon, wither, blaze) is interpolation only; water and lava flags come from the live mob; other mob-specific client `onLivingUpdate` code (spider climbing flag, slime, enderman) is not run.
- A mount or rider is still drawn at its live position (the client player is not simulated riding the client mount); other mobs in a leash are held at the live position.
- Items, XP orbs, arrows and particles still use live positions; their tracker entries (update delays 10/20, ranges 64/160) are not modelled.
- The client player's own entity and `onEntityUpdate` side effects on the client are not run.
