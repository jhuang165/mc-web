# Client hits, client sounds, tracker iteration order (batch 45, branch batch45-clienthits)

## Summary
- **Client arrow hits (executed).** `TrackerHitsProbe` now lists real client-world mobs (a cow and an enderman with `attackEntityFrom` logged, the client player with `PlayerCapabilities.disableDamage`) and runs the unchanged classes. The client `EntityArrow` searches the client world's entities (not under `isRemote`), draws its damage (a crit also draws `nextInt`: 4 became 5 in the recorded run), calls `attackEntityFrom`, which answers false on a client world, so the arrow ricochets at a tenth of its speed and keeps flying. A creative client player is dropped as a hit. The port: `Arrows.step` with `ctx.remote` draws the damage, hands it to `ctx.attack` and always ricochets; `ObjectTracker.clientHitContext` supplies the targets (`hooks.clientTargets`, wired in `main.js` from the client mob copies and the player) and the shooter skip (`hooks.clientOf` from the live arrow's shooter).
- **Client hook hits (executed).** The client `EntityFishHook` searches entities too and calls `attackEntityFrom(thrown damage 0)` on a client cow (logged twice on the recorded path); it never attaches (`hooked` stays unset) because the call returns false. The port's `FishHook` searches under `env.remote` and calls `hitEntity`, which is false for client copies.
- **Thrown entities: no search (bytecode-read and executed).** `EntityThrowable.onUpdate` searches under `!isRemote`; `snowballMob` and `pearlMob` fly through a listed cow with no `attackEntityFrom` call. The port already had no targets for them; now replay-covered.
- **Client sounds (executed, call order with the particles).** Logged client-world sounds: `random.bowhit` on a stuck arrow, `game.neutral.swim.splash` on water entry (before the bubbles and splash particles, as in `handleWaterMovement`), the swim and `step.*` sounds from the stepping hook (func_145780_a: `step.stone` on a stone floor), `fireworks.launch` from the rocket after its first move. They are logged by `ObjectTracker.sound`. **Lead correction:** the track first played them through `hooks.sound` in `main.js` (a doubled sound). The lead read `RenderGlobal.playSound` (`bma.a(String,DDDFF)`) with javap: its body is a bare `return`, and `WorldClient` (`bjf`) overrides only the positional `playSound(x,y,z,...,boolean)`, so a client copy's `Entity.playSound` / `playSoundAtEntity` is silent in 1.7.10. `main.js` now binds no sound hook; the log stays as call-order evidence. Only the server's copies (in `Arrows`/`Projectiles` events) are heard.
- **EntityTracker iteration order (bytecode-read only, not executed; port not changed).** `mn` (EntityTracker) keeps `trackedEntities` in a `java.util.HashSet<my>`; `my.hashCode()` returns the entity id and `equals` compares ids. `updateTrackedEntities` (`mn.a()`) and the player-list pass iterate it, so entries go out in bucket order (id spread `h ^ h>>>16` masked by a table of 16 that doubles past a 0.75 load), not in spawn or world-list order. The port walks the live list (a comment now says so in `serverTick`). Not changed: mobs in the port carry no shared sequential entity id, and the order only changes how packets of different entities interleave within one tick (each entity's own stream is identical).
