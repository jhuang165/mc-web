- **Blasts reach more entities.** Native `Explosion.doExplosionA` takes every entity in the box except the exploder. Wither skulls and arrows were not asked for; arrows were already pushed.
- **Thrown balls hit hanging entities.** `EntityThrowable`'s ray takes any `canBeCollidedWith` entity: paintings and item frames are now in the targets of the player's thrown snowballs, eggs, pearls and potions (`main.js`), witch/dispenser potions and golem snowballs (`vehicleTargets` in `entity-simulation.js`). A snowball, egg or pearl breaks the painting (`attackEntityFrom(thrown, 0)`; a frame holding an item only loses the item, a creative thrower gets no drop); a potion shatters on it and leaves it standing. Records `snowball-painting` and `potion-painting`: the hit tick and the painting item joining the end of the list in that pass match. Player-thrown balls and the egg/pearl branch are wired by reading `projectileHit` / `projectilePearlImpact` in main.js (not executed; the probe has no thrower). Player-thrown balls still do not target carts or boats (unchanged, see follow-ups).
- **Vehicle pushes on mobs run in the mob's update.** `EntityLivingBase.onLivingUpdate` ends with `collideWithNearbyEntities`, which calls the cart's or boat's `applyEntityCollision`. `EntityWorld.push` now calls `pushMob` of the cart and boat walkers (`pushMobOnVehicles`, `structure-simulation.js`) at the mob's position before a rider's seat is taken; `finishMinecartSimulation`/`finishBoatSimulation` no longer push mobs (the player's push and the drops stay after the walk). Records `cart-cow-*`, `boat-cow-*`, `cart-rider-push-*` (a cow riding one cart pushed by another) replay exactly in both list orders; this closes the "rider cow pushed by another cart is not replayable" gap.
- **Bug the probe found.** `Boats.collide` took the square root of the larger axis in double; `MathHelper.sqrt_double` is a float, so now `f(Math.sqrt(m))` (differences of about 1e-9 in the cow's push, `boat-cow-*`).
- Native evidence is executed (unchanged `Explosion`, `EntityMinecartEmpty`, `EntityBoat`, `EntityPainting`, `EntityPotion`, `EntitySnowball`, cow); member names were bytecode-read as in batch 44.
