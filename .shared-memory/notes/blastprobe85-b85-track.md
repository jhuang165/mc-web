# Batch 85 blastprobe85: explosion facts, executed

## Summary
- **BlastOrderProbe** (`tools/probes/BlastOrderProbe.java`, run by `node tools/verify-blast-order.mjs`, writes `public/research/blast-order-reference.json`) now holds 1300 records: the 400 b83 `blast` scenes (unchanged) and 300 each of `dragon`, `hang`, `crystal`. Each new scene logs every hit event (`h`), dragon body hits (`bd`), `attackEntityFromPart` calls with the damage passed and the health after (`pt`), and items spawned through `World.spawnEntityInWorld` while an entity was being hurt (`drop`). It also records the push alone (constructor motion zeroed) and which entities are dead after.
- **Dragon (executed, 300 scenes).** The real `EntityDragon` (xa) in the real chunk lists, its seven parts placed at chosen positions with the tick-time sizes (3x3, 5x3, 2x2 x3, 4x2, 4x3; the constructor's 6/8/4 are overwritten by `xa.e` every tick). The port's `mobRows` matches native on: which parts are listed (a part can be hit while the body, judged by its centre, is out of reach), the body hit and push, each part's damage, non-head damage `d/4 + 1`, the hurt window across parts (the second part is refused unless it exceeds the first's damage), nested crystal blasts hitting the parts again, the flight target the hit draws (h, i, bm vs `s.tx/ty/tz`), and the dragon's `Random` state after the blast (same draws). One divergence fixed: native health is clamped to [0, max] (`setHealth`); `dragon.js livingHurt` let it go negative (now `Math.max(0, ...)`).
- **Hanging (executed, 300 scenes).** Real EntityPainting, EntityItemFrame (item, no item, item with drop chance 0) and EntityLeashKnot with items, crystals, TNT and orbs, the exploder none or a TNT placed by a survival or a creative player. Executed facts: a frame holding an item loses the item (it drops at the frame's turn unless the chance roll fails) and survives; a second hit (a nested blast) breaks the frame and drops the frame item; a painting drops at its turn; a knot dies with no drop; every drop is spawned at the hanging entity's own turn, not after the blast. Port divergence fixed: a **creative player's TNT** breaks a painting or frame with no drop (`EntityDamageSource "explosion.player"` -> `onBroken(player)`: creative returns, and a framed item is cleared without a drop); `Hanging.explodeOne` took no source, so it always dropped. It now takes `by` and `nonLivingRows` passes `{player, creative}` when the attacker is a creative player (single player). The dedicated server's `nonLivingRows` call still passes no creative flag (server TNT has no placer record, see Follow-ups). The probe's frame geometry and painting art ordinals equal the port's (`hangingGeometry`, `ARTS`).
- **Crystal / eye / hook (executed, 300 scenes).** Crystals, items, eyes of ender and fishing hooks on the reach gate (distance / (2 * size) from 0.55 to 1.0, exactly 1.0 included), 1.0-6.0 sizes. The port's push matches native to the last bit for the crystal (eye = posY, box 2x2x2 per b84), the real `EntityEnderEye` (zd, yOffset 0, 0.25 box) and the real `EntityFishHook` (xe). Mutation check: setting the crystal eye back to posY + 1.7 fails the crystal test. Limit: the probe chunks hold no blocks, so exposure density is 1 and the box size is not exercised through partial exposure.
- **Native fact kept as a test note:** `Explosion.doExplosionA` has no isDead test; a dead entity (an item killed by the first blast) still sits in the chunk list that tick and a nested blast pushes it again, with no hit logged (the probe's hit hook logs only alive entities). The port skips it. Unobservable: the entity is gone next tick. The replay compares motion only for entities alive afterwards.
- **Away run, eyes and hooks (decision, no code).** The away run (`away-entities.js`) has no eye system and no angler: dimension switch stores `eyes.serialize()` in the dimension record, nothing ticks it (`eyes.tick` runs only in the live dimension, `main.js`), so an eye in an away dimension is a frozen saved record. Natively an eye in a loaded chunk of another dimension would keep flying and shatter or drop in 80 ticks; the port has no such tick, so pushing a frozen record would be half a model. Left unwired. Hooks (bytecode-read, `xe.h`, EntityFishHook.onUpdate): `!world.isRemote` then `angler.isDead || !angler.isEntityAlive() || heldItem == null || item != fishing_rod || distanceSq(angler) > 1024` -> `setDead` and return; `EntityList` has no hook entry so a hook is never saved. A hook without its angler therefore dies at its next update and cannot be in a blast of an away world. Executed instead: live-world eye and hook pushes (above).
