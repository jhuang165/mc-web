# passivedraws (batch 44)

## Summary
Every EntityAITasks mob now draws its tasks on its own `Entity.rand`: `Brain` takes `ai.deps.entityRandom(e)` as its default random (cow, mooshroom, pig, horse, ocelot, iron golem, snow golem, witch, creeper and anything else that had `ai.rand`). Task lists, priorities and draw order were checked against the constructors with javap (bytecode-read): EntityCow (wh), EntityMooshroom (wm), EntityPig (wo), EntityHorse (wi), EntityOcelot (wn), EntityIronGolem (wt), EntitySnowman (wr), EntityWitch (yp), EntityCreeper (xz), EntityAnimal (wf), EntityAgeable (rx), and the tasks EntityAIMate (ua), Tempt (vk), FollowParent (uh), Wander (vc), WatchClosest (un), LookIdle (vb), EatGrass (ud), AvoidEntity (tw), RunAroundLikeCrazy (vg), OcelotSit (uw), Panic (uz). The horse got its own list (swim, panic, mate, follow parent, wander, watch closest, look idle) in `ai.js`.

Native evidence: 52 new `b44pd-*` scenarios in `tools/probes/EndermanWolfScenarios.java` (`run44`, `EW_SET=b44`) run the unchanged classes on the b42/b43 harness and write `public/research/enderman-wolf-b44-reference.json`; `src/enderman-wolf-b44.test.js` replays all 52 with no divergence (position, yaw, health, target, flags with [inLove, growingAge], every task's draws per tick, motion, sounds). Cases: idle far and near for 10 mob types, panic, tempt (cow, pig, ocelot with fish), breeding (cow, mooshroom, pig x2, cow with pig, ocelot), follow parent, wolf taming with bones (two scenarios, `nextInt(3)`), wolf feeding (also sitting), wolf mating (three), ocelot taming, creeper and ocelot avoid, ocelot sit on a bed, iron golem and snow golem against a zombie, witch against the player, creeper watching. Players hold wheat, carrots, raw fish or bones through the real `EntityPlayer.interactWith` (`use` op). The probe world sets `brightnessLevel` (15).

Behaviour changes the records forced (all native):
- EntityAnimal.onLivingUpdate order: love and age count down after the AI, move, push and loot; every 10th love tick draws 3 gaussians and 3 floats; a non-adult loses love. The parents age once in the tick they mate (5999 after the spawn), so `entities.test.js` and `crops.test.js` now expect 5999.
- Mate.spawnBaby draws 7 heart argument sets and the orb's `nextInt(7)+1` on the mother's `Entity.rand`; feeding hearts (state 18) use a client-only stream; wolf and ocelot taming draw their 7 argument sets on the server stream even with no fx.
- Horse: `nextInt(200)` before the AI, then `nextInt(900)` (heal 1, unconditional draw) and `nextInt(300)` (eat grass, 50 ticks); rearing and eating block movement and the AI and do not advance the task counter (`e.aiSkipped`); the hurt sound draws `nextInt(3)` first and 0 rears (20 ticks).
- Ocelot: sprinting (move speed 1.33) adds the 0.3 operation 2 speed modifier, the sprint jump boost (0.2 along yaw, MathHelper table sin/cos), and two `nextFloat` draws a tick for the sprint particle; AvoidEntity for the player is a sphere (`getClosestPlayerToEntity`), not a box; Tempt compares player pitch and yaw in degrees and starts them at 0.
- Landing (fall damage and step sounds) is emitted before the step sound at the end of `moveEntity`.
- The probe world now shows a spawned animal baby to collisions for the tick it is spawned (as `spawnEntityInWorld` does); the replay does the same and neither ticks it afterwards. The replay clears the snow golem's snowballs after each tick (the probe never flies them).
