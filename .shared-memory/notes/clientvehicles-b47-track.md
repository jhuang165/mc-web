# clientvehicles b47 track (claude-b47-clientvehicles)

Branch `batch47-clientvehicles` from 3250e20f5 (0.75.0).

## Summary
- Boats, minecarts, paintings, item frames and leash knots now have client copies in `src/object-tracker.js` (new kinds `boat`, `minecart`, `painting`, `itemFrame`, `leashKnot`), fed by `hooks.extraLists` (bound in `bindObjectTracker`, the only `src/main.js` edit). They are the client's arrow and hook hit targets (`ObjectTracker.vehicleTargets`, used by `hooks.clientTargets`) instead of the live entities.
- Executed (unchanged 1.7.10 classes through `tools/probes/TrackerVehiclesProbe.java` -> `public/research/tracker-vehicles-reference.json`, 21 cases + `dragonPartHit`): the entry's packets for EntityBoat, EntityMinecart (chest, TNT, rideable), EntityPainting, EntityItemFrame, EntityLeashKnot (S0E 1/10/71/77, S10, S1C, S12, S15/S17/S18); the client classes built from them and ticked: the boat spawns with an extra yOffset (y + 0.3F), interpolates 3 + 5 = 8 ticks then coasts on the last S12 (drag 0.99F/0.95F/0.99F, float constants); the cart does not lift, interpolates 3 + 2 = 5 ticks and never moves on its own; hanging copies are static, their box is `hangingGeometry` from the S0E/S10 tile (tile * 32, not the entity position; the frame uses 9 px, the painting its art by title, the knot a 0.5 cube); the item frame's S1C (item index 2, rotation 3); the metadata of boat (17-19) and cart (17-22). Replayed tick by tick in `src/object-tracker-vehicles.test.js` (positions, motion, previous position, rotation, boxes, watcher, every packet).
- Executed, dragon: `EntityDragon.attackEntityFromPart` on the client dragon draws three `Entity.rand` floats (x, y, z order) for its new target: x = posX + sin(yaw)*5 + (r - 0.5)*2, y = posY + r*3 + 1, z = posZ - cos(yaw)*5 + (r - 0.5)*2 (`dragonPartHit` rows, four yaws, two hits each). `ObjectTracker.retargetDragon` (called from `attack` for a `dragonPart` target) does the draws on the client dragon's `rand` and keeps `ref.target`. The main.js client dragon ref is the ClientEntity (it has `rand`), so the draws advance its stream.
- Bytecode-read, not executed: the boat's splash particles above 0.2625 blocks a tick (the probe's boat is slowed below that by its own drag; no client splash draws are modelled), the furnace cart's S1C index 16, a cart below y -64, the `isBoatEmpty` full-physics branch.
- What draws from where: the renderers (boat, minecart, painting, frame, knot) still draw the LIVE entities; switching them to the copies needs edits outside `bindObjectTracker` (`boatRenderer.update`, `minecartRenderer.update`, hanging scene lists, shadows). Hit TARGETS and arrow/hook behaviour use the client copies. The client copy of a boat or cart the client player rides is not modelled (it runs full physics natively): the live entity stays the target then (`rider.vehicle === live`).
- Port structure difference: the port's entry starts at the first tick a live entity is listed (with that tick's motion); the executed run adds the entity to the tracker at creation (zero motion) and pushes it afterwards, so natively a pushed boat sends S12(0,0,0) then the S12 with the motion. The replay creates the entry first to match.
- Unexplained: `boatFar` (range 80, player 82.7 blocks away) - the executed entry never sent a destroy by tick 79, while the port's entry untracks at the 4-block re-check on tick 71. The replay stops at tick 71. Not investigated further (probably my probe's player/watch setup; the port's range logic is the one verified for mobs in batch 40).
- Edited outside the new test: `src/entity-tracker.js` (spawn packet position uses `e.tile * 32` when the tracked entity carries a `tile`), `tools/probes/TrackerItemsProbe.java` (cases 1, 10, 71, 77, S10), `tools/probes/TrackerVehiclesProbe.java`, `tools/verify-tracker-vehicles.mjs`. Untouched: server/, src/multiplayer/, PROJECT.md, remaining-gaps.md, other tracks' files.
