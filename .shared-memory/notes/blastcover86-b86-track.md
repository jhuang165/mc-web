# Batch 86 blastcover86: explosion exposure through blocks, the server's Creative TNT, dispensed TNT

## Summary
- **Cover scenes (executed, 600).** `tools/probes/BlastOrderProbe.java` gained case `cover`: the probe world (`BW`) now answers `getBlock` / `getBlockMetadata` from a per-scene block map, so the unchanged `agw.a()` runs `World.getBlockDensity` (`ahb.a(azw, azt)`) through real block bounds. Each scene puts 1-3 block kinds from a 110-kind palette (stone, glass, stained glass, leaves, ice, water and lava at several levels, fire, slabs top and bottom, fences, nether fence, cobblestone wall, panes, iron bars, wooden and iron doors in both halves, double plants, stairs, gates, trapdoors, torches, ladders, buttons, levers, plates, rails, signs, chests, beds, cake, cauldron, brewing stand, end portal frame, portal, skulls, flower pots, plants, crops, stems, vines, cocoa, snow, carpet, anvil, beacon, hopper, dragon egg and more) as scatter, walls with gaps, a shell, or walls plus scatter, around 2-6 entities: items, orbs, arrows, primed TNT, eyes of ender, fishing hooks, zombies, pigs (adult and baby), spiders, cows, an ender crystal (nested blast) and a player with EntityPlayerMP geometry (`mw.<init>`: yOffset 0, eye 1.62). The probe logs the hit order and depth, the damage passed to every living entity and crystal (`dmg`), the push and who is dead. `src/blastcover86-b86.test.js` replays every scene through `applyExplosion` over a port world built with `fromOriginal`: **0 mismatches** after the fixes below. Coverage: every one of the 13 entity kinds sees partial exposure (more than 5 scenes) and zero exposure (more than 3); 100+ block kinds appear.
- **Ray-box divergences fixed (found by the cover scenes and a scratch single-block ray probe, 40 rays x every palette id x meta 0-15).**
  - `projectiles.js` `RAY_BOUND_IDS` lacked the end portal (149), pumpkin and melon stems (159, 160), trapped and ender chests (162, 163), portal (164), nether wart (170), dragon egg (171) and skull (172): `rayTraceBlocks` hit them as full cubes. They now use `blockBounds`, which already had their `setBlockBoundsBasedOnState` boxes. This also changes arrows, thrown entities and every other `rayTraceBlocks` user.
  - `world.js` mushrooms (75/76): the bounds were doubles (.3/.4/.7); native are floats (0.30000001192092896, 0.4000000059604645, 0.699999988079071).
  - `world.js` vine meta 0 (bytecode-read `aoh.a(ahl,III)` and `aoh.a(aji)`): the top plate `[0, 15/16, 0, 1, 1, 1]` only when the block above passes `func_150093_a` (`renderAsNormalBlock && material.blocksMovement`, the port's `normalCube`); otherwise the bounds stay min 1 / max 0 and no ray hits. The port always gave the top plate.
- **Native facts the probe pins rather than the port (documented, not modelled).** Block bounds are shared mutable fields, so some ray boxes depend on history. The end portal frame (`aku`) keeps the constructor's full cube until `g()` (setBlockBoundsForItemRender) or its `addCollisionBoxesToList` (which ends in `g()`) runs, then it is 13/16 high for good; the probe calls `aji.e(120).g()` once to put it in that steady state, which the port models. A button with an invalid facing (meta 0, 5-7 and their powered twins) keeps whatever bounds the last block left; the palette uses valid facings only.
- **Dedicated server, Creative TNT (wired).** `BlockTNT.onBlockActivated` (`aob.a(ahb,III,yz,IFFF)`, bytecode-read): flint and steel in hand primes the block through `func_150114_a(meta 1, player)`, sets it to air and calls `damageItem(1, player)`. The server had no such branch: the click fell through to `ItemFlintAndSteel.onItemUse` and lit the face cell, so a player's TNT burnt with no placer. `multiplayer/simulation.js edit` now primes with `{ placer: p }` (sound `game.tnt.primed`, no wear in Creative, not when sneaking with an item). A burning arrow (`aob.a(ahb,III,sa)`: `zc.c instanceof sv`) now passes `ownerOf(arrow)` (`server-dispense.js` `ctx.tntPlacer`, `igniteBlockTnt(x, y, z, placer)`). `server-mobs.js explode` passes `creative: placer.survival.creative` to `nonLivingRows` when the placer is a server player, so paintings, item frames, minecarts and boats drop nothing in a Creative player's blast, as in single player (blastprobe85). Chained TNT keeps the placer (`aob.a(ahb,III,agw)`: `agw.c()`).
- **Dispensed TNT (bytecode-read, no change).** `kl` (Bootstrap) registers `kr` for `Item.getItemFromBlock(Blocks.tnt)`; `kr.b(ck, add)` builds `new xw(world, x + 0.5F, y + 0.5F, z + 0.5F, null)`: dispensed TNT has **no placer**. Powered TNT (`aob.b(ahb,IIII)` -> `a(..., null)`) and burnt TNT have none either. The port's single player (`main.js spawnTnt`, `placer: null`) and server (`server-dispense.js host().spawnTnt`) already match; a test pins the server's.
- **Single-player fireball look (fixed, found on the way).** `Fireballs.lookOf("player")` returned `mobs.playerLook()`, which `main.js` gives as `{ yaw, pitch }` in degrees, and `deflect` indexes it as a vector: a large fireball in the player's TNT blast got NaN motion. It now converts with `lookVector` (EntityLivingBase.getLook(1.0F)); a replay that hands a vector keeps it.

## Follow-ups
- The port files its player by the eye (`stampPlayerCell`); EntityPlayerMP files by posY = feet. The cover replay keeps the probe player's feet and eye in one vertical slice to avoid the difference. A player whose feet and eye straddle a slice boundary can be hit at a different turn than natively.
- Server fireballs: a large fireball in a server player's TNT blast takes no look and no owner (`b.placer` is null for a server player record; `Fireballs.lookOf` and the owner fields expect a mob or `"player"`). Natively it turns to the player's look and the player owns it.
- Server mobs killed by a player's TNT: mobRows still gets the TNT as attacker, not "explosion.player" with the player, so no player kill credit (recentlyHit, XP) on the server.
- Server skeleton arrows lighting TNT pass no placer (only player shooters are resolved by `ownerOf`).
- `ocelot-ai.js` reads `playerLook()` as radians (`v.yaw * DEG`) while `main.js` gives degrees; not checked here.
- History-dependent native bounds (end portal frame before any collision or item render, buttons with invalid facings, the brewing stand before a collision pass) are not modelled beyond the steady states.
- Browser-only: how the pushes and damage through cover look in play; the flint-and-steel click on a server TNT block in a real client (swing, sound, the block update), and the client's prediction of that click.
