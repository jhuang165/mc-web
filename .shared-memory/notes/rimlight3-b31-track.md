# rimlight3 b31 track (rim of the loaded chunk set, 64-change resend)

## Summary
- Native probe: `tools/probes/RimLightProbe.java` + `tools/verify-rim-light.mjs` -> `public/research/rim-light-reference.json` (unchanged 1.7.10 `World`/`Chunk` light code; terrain over chunks -7..7 loaded chunk by chunk with `ChunkProviderServer.populate` and a server tick per load; 9x9, 13x13, 15x15 sets, spiral and x-major order, seeds 311/312, plus a Nether record with `resetRelightChecks` and 600 more ticks). Per-chunk digests of sky, block light and heights for all 225 chunks. `NativeLight` (`src/native-light.js`) replays all five records exactly through the new helper `src/rim-light-ref.js`, so it is the reference for per-cell comparison.
- Finding: the outermost loaded chunks (an unloaded chunk among the eight neighbours) hold `generateSkylightMap` sky (vertical only) of the final blocks and no block light: 4 to 29 sky and 2 to 24 block cells per chunk differ (Overworld), against 66 to 164 sky and 13 to 240 block for the batch 29/30 barrier model. Nether rim block light: 36 to 195 cells differ against about 7,700 to 8,000.
- Port: `ring-light.js` `isRim` + `verticalLight`; `RingLight.at/light/search` in `headless-columns.js` answer rim columns from the vertical light at once (no budget), turn off the emitters of rim chunks in the bulk light and the per-cell search of their neighbours, and `invalidateLight` covers 5 x 5 chunks. `ring.noRim` restores the old model for tests.
- Not ported, measured: depth 1 and deeper depend on whether population ran with every chunk within 17 blocks loaded (load order); native block light stays darker than the fixed point there by 100 to 640 cells a chunk, permanently. Same family as batch 20's "fresh chunks are lit eagerly", left as it was. Nether interior (even 600 ticks after load) differs by 6,700 to 8,400 block cells a chunk because `enqueueRelightChecks` does 8 slots a tick.
- Client light probe: added `resend64` (PlayerInstance sends a partial chunk packet at 64 changed cells; the client copies the flagged sections and never runs those setBlocks). 3,983 block cells in the unflagged neighbour chunk stay dark on the client (max 14), client ticks heal 255 in 600. Not ported (needs the second light volume). Population spill is argued, not probed (see light-engine.md "Batch 31").
