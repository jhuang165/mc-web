# Neighbors3 (batch 31) track

## Summary
- Powered doors: `nativeNeighborWorldClass` no longer throws for a door that is powered or is told by a power-providing neighbour (`doorSetOpen`, BlockDoor.func_150014_a, bytecode-read). It reads the combined state like func_150012_g, does nothing when the state already matches, and otherwise flips bit 4 of the lower half (flags 2). Pinned by the 80 native `neighbour/power` records of `doors-panes-reference.json` (DoorPaneProbe, unchanged BlockDoor), replayed in the new `src/native-neighbor-world.test.js` (both halves as the changed cell). The power providers that used to throw (lever, wire, torch, repeater, hook, rails, comparators, ...) are read through the native-replayed `redstone.js` `weak` / `strong` behaviours on the area's own block store; plates and buttons keep the earlier verified code. A door that loses its support now clears both halves and drops the door item (same order as the quiet `structure-world-blocks.js` door) instead of throwing.
- How often the fallback fires: 0. A bounded survey (`artifacts/b31-neighbors3/survey.mjs`: streamed default worlds at generation 20, seeds 1-6, 80 x 80 chunks each, 600 populate cells, 38,400 chunks, plus seeds 120, 671, 17, 579, 9, 126 at 32 x 32 chunks) ended with zero `retried without neighbour callbacks` warnings and zero unpopulated-fallback errors, both on the base commit (`2d4287be`) and on this branch. The 50 native flat areas and 9 native structure areas run `PopulationArea` directly in neighbours mode, so a throw would fail their tests: none throws. So no native population area produces a powered-door chain; the door port is pinned by the native door records, not by a population record. Vanilla generation places doors with neighbour source = the door itself, and flag-2 writes for everything else, so a toggle is out of reach of generated content.
- Still retried quietly (not hit by any area): a fallen button (its drop order is unported) and a powered TNT block.
- Imported saves: new generations 22 (MapFeatures on) and 23 (off) = 16 / 17 plus the neighbour callbacks, with the import origin (`nativeSpawnGeneration` is false for them). `levelGenerator` (level.dat imports) returns 22 / 23. Decision and evidence: a native save populates unpopulated chunks through the same `ChunkProviderGenerate.populate` with the block callbacks, and a stored 6 x 6 area around the seed 671 jungle temple populates with different cells at 22 than at 16 (new test in `imported-population.test.js`, 25 populates, no failed or unmapped cell). Worlds already imported or saved at 16 / 17 keep their gate: their stored columns are untouched and their unvisited terrain regenerates with quiet callbacks as before. `region-import.js` was not touched (its `generationVersion` override still lists 13 to 17).
- Default-world jungle decoration "difference": not a port bug any more. The batch 14 note (chunk 0,14 of `jungleTemple671`, flower and tree call streams) is the pre-16 mutated-biome map: with `legacyMutated: true` the replay diverges at chunk (0,14) exactly as recorded (ordered writes 3000 expected), with the native rule the full area replays every chunk call for call (writes, both Randoms, final chunk hashes, tiles, carts, pending ticks). `structure-live-neighbors.test.js` now compares all jungle chunks; the old exemption is gone. Only worlds saved at 13 or 15 keep the legacy map, by design. No additional native jungle area was generated (a biome scan was too slow here); jungle decoration is also covered by the 79-region audit and the six flat jungle areas.
