# Batch 48 deathdrops track

Branch `batch48-deathdrops` (from 61e069a4a, release 0.76.0). Labels: executed = real 1.7.10 classes run in a probe and replayed; bytecode-read = read from the jar, not run; port = port-only design.

## Summary
- **Death loot in the dying entity's own turn.** `EntityWorld.flushLoot` (src/entities.js) runs at the step of every entry of the one walk in `tickMobs` (also after a `continue`, before the length is read again). It turns the queued `drop` and `xp` events (never a bottle's orbs) into items and orbs through a host hook `mobs.spawnLoot` (`spawnLoot` in src/living-simulation.js), which main.js and the away run (src/away-entities.js) set. The new entity joins through the usual `joined` stamps and is walked later in the same pass. Without the hook the events wait for `drain()` as before. This covers every `drop`/`xp` event raised in an entry's turn: kill drops (fall, fire, arrow, a mob's hit), deathTime-20 orbs, breeding orbs, shearing, egg laying. (Port design; native behaviour executed, see below.)
- **Executed:** `EntityListProbe` grew from 74 to 78 cases: `death-fall-cow-hit`, `-nohit`, `-item-after`, `-item-before` (a real EntityCow with 1 health falls and dies; recentlyHit 100 or 0). Native: its items spawn and update in the death pass (age 1, pickup delay 9 at the landing tick); its two orbs appear at deathTime 20 (none with recentlyHit 0). The port replays all four exactly; `without "lootInTurn"` (the old after-walk drain) diverges.
- **Executed side effects found by the records:** items and orbs spawned at a mob's feet sit half in the floor, so their first update pushes them out sideways with `Entity.rand`. The probe now seeds every item and orb `Entity.rand` with Random(7) (old 74 records are byte-identical, and the 78 repeat across runs). The replay gives each item and orb its own Random(7) for that push. `ItemEntities.move` now keeps the item box between ticks and reads `posX/posZ = (min + max) / 2` as `Entity.moveEntity` does (the drift differed by one unit in the last place after three ticks).
- **Live carts, boats, hanging entities:** main.js sets `boats.joined`, `minecarts.joined` and `hanging.joined` (classes already called `this.joined?.()`), so a placed cart, boat, painting or frame takes its native list place. Unit-tested with the replay-style harness plus a source pin of the three lines (main.js itself has no unit harness).
- **Exp bottle:** `WitchPotions.expImpact` draws `3 + nextInt(5) + nextInt(5)` from `mobs.worldRandom` (World.rand: weather.random live and away; Random(2) in the probe replay) instead of the ball's own generator. The break sound's pitch keeps the ball generator (a client-side draw). Bytecode-read for World.rand; the pthrow-expbottle records still replay exactly.
- Bytecode-read only: onDeath's loot happens during the hurt call (not at the end of the hurt entity's turn). The port spawns at the end of the entity's step in the walk, so an entity that spawns something else later in the same step (a chick, the zombie of a villager kill) is listed before the loot. No probe orders two spawns in one turn.

## Follow-ups (proposed gap lines)
- Death loot spawns at the end of the dying entry's step, not at the hurt call; the order against another spawn in the same step (egg chick, a hatched or converted mob) is unprobed.
- Port items and orbs share one random (`ItemEntities.random`, `ExperienceOrbs.random`) where native entities each have `Entity.rand`; only the death records' replay swaps in per-entity Random(7).
- Mob `drop`/`xp` events outside the walk (a player's kill, `/kill`, explosions) still drain after the tick; the item first ticks next pass, as native for a hit before the walk, unprobed for a hit after it.
- The break-sound pitch of an exp bottle (client side) still uses the ball's generator.
- Live `main.js` carts/boats/hanging hooks are source-pinned only; no in-browser placement check of the stamp order.
- Item entity box keeps x/z only (`bxz`); y still derives from `e.y`.
