0.21.3 (commit 150968f, pushed to github main; not published to the Site). Ports the tail of Entity.moveEntity as one function, blockCollisions in the new src/block-collisions.js. It replaces the per-system cactus, web and fire-contact checks.

What it does:
- doBlockCollisions [sa.I]: each cactus cell in the box contracted by 0.001 deals cactus 1, so two cells are two hits; webs call setInWeb, which flying players and spiders skip.
- isBoundingBoxBurning: fire or lava. Inside it, inFire 1 and, unless wet, ++fire, with setFire(8) when the counter reaches 0. Outside it, a counter at or below 0 rests at -fireResistance (1, or 20 for EntityPlayer).
- Wet and burning: random.fizz and the counter resets to -fireResistance.

Wiring:
- Player: tickSurvival calls it right after survival.tick (the onEntityUpdate part), with the post-move box. survival.tick now zeroes fire only for water, not rain.
- Mobs: moveEntity/moveEntityWithHeading take a hooks argument, passed by walk. burn() lost its fire/cactus contact and now zeroes fire only for water.
- Items: move(world, e, noClip, wet). noClip skips the tail, as in the original.
- XP orbs: gained the burn countdown, lava (4 damage, 300 fire) and 5 health, and die at 0.
- Falling blocks and TNT get webs only, as before.

Behaviour changes:
- The player stands 20 ticks in fire or lava before catching.
- Items die on their first lava tick (lava 4 plus inFire 1).
- Lightning no longer ignites a resting player (-20 + 1 is not 0) but does ignite mobs.
- Rain puts mobs out with a fizz.
- Cactus destroys XP orbs.
