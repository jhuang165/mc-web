# Wither boss (Minecraft Java 1.7.10)

Not full parity. Evidence: `tools/probes/WitherBossProbe.java` runs the unchanged EntityWither (`xc`), EntityWitherSkull (`zp`) and EntityFireball (`ze`) on the MobMoveProbe world double; `tools/verify-wither-boss.mjs` writes `public/research/wither-boss-reference.json` (record counts: facts 1, spawnStart 2, spawn 440, headPos 4, launchEntity 2, launchCoords 4, skullStart 2, skullFlight 60, skullImpact 9, skullBlast 24, skullProps 1, hurt 3, armored 6, drops 4, nbt 1); `src/wither-boss.test.js` checks the port against it.

## Behaviour

- Construction: soul sand T plus three wither skulls calls `spawnWither`: Invul 220, health max/3 (100), +10 health every 10 ticks, size growth, then a power 7 explosion at the eye height and the `mob.wither.spawn` broadcast (level event 1013). During the countdown nothing else runs (probe: `xc.bn`).
- EntityWither: 300 HP, 0.9 x 4, flying chase (motionY * 0.6, climb to 5 blocks above the target while unarmoured), three heads (centre follows the attack target, side heads pick their own targets), 1 HP regeneration per 20 ticks, armour phase at or below 150 HP that rejects arrows, armour value 4, undead, immune to fire, drowning and falls, rejects undead attackers, blockBreakCounter sweep (3x4x3 around it, skipping bedrock, end portal, frame and command block), nether star drop (2060) and 50 XP.
- EntityWitherSkull: black and blue (0.3125 box), motion factor 0.95 / 0.73, explosion power 1 without fire, the blue skull caps block resistance at 0.8 except bedrock, end portal, frame and command block, wither effect 10 s on Normal and 40 s on Hard, 8 damage, heals the shooter 5 on a kill.
- Rendering: ModelWither (three heads, shoulders, spine, tail), wither.png, wither_invulnerable.png flashing in the last 80 spawn ticks, growth scale, scrolling armour layer below half health, skull billboard, fullbright.
- `src/boss-bar.js`: reusable `setBossStatus(entity, name)` (health / max, 100 frames), a DOM bar from `icons.png` with its own animation loop; the dragon track can call it.
- `/summon WitherBoss` with `Invul`; the region export writes `WitherBoss` with an Invul int and the importer restores it.

## Evidence

- Probe-run (unchanged classes): facts, spawn countdown and explosion/event, head positions, skull launch positions and aux 1014, skull flight, skull impact explosion, block resistance, hurt rejections (invulnerable, drown, armoured arrow), armour threshold, drops, Invul NBT.
- Bytecode-derived (`javap -c -p xc`, `xc.bn`/`xc.e`, `zp.a(azu)`, `bpc`, `bin`, `bln`, `bbv`): head cadence (10 + rand 10 ticks, random shots every 16th on Normal/Hard, 40 + rand 20 after a targeted shot), target selection, head tracking (40 / 10 degrees), block breaking, regeneration, the wither effect and damage values, model boxes, boss bar layout.

## Gaps

- `xc.bn` after the invulnerable phase and `xc.e` flight call the full EntityMob stack, so they are bytecode-derived, not probed; the port runs it as the Brain's updateAITick (after the navigator, before the move helpers), not after all of super.bn.
- Probed (batch 15, `WitherBossProbe` cases skullMob 16 and witherDot 8): the skull impact on a cow and zombie by difficulty (8 damage as a mob source, effect amplifier 1 for 0/0/200/800 ticks on Peaceful/Easy/Normal/Hard, zombie 7.36 after armour), the shooter heal of 5 on a kill, and `Potion.wither` damage over time on non-player mobs (1.0 per `duration % (40 >> amplifier)`, bypassing armour; EntityWither ignores the effect). The wither skeleton's melee effect now ticks on mobs through the same path.
- Batch 16 re-read `xc.bn` (javap): the head candidate pool is `selectEntitiesWithinAABB(EntityLivingBase, box.expand(20,8,20), bp)`, up to 10 tries of `rand.nextInt(size)`, skip self/dead/unseen (removed from the list), a player with disableDamage ends the search, else `setWatchedTarget`; this matches `witherAITick`. The candidate order (chunk x, chunk z, 16-high slice, insertion order, undead excluded) was probed in batch 17. Not modelled: an entity that crossed a slice or chunk is re-appended natively; the engine here orders by current position.
- Head particles use the client entity's own Random (probed in batch 17, see below). The spawn snow particles are not shown: `BlockSkull` calls `spawnParticle` on the server world, which is a no-op, so the native game never shows them either. The kill-Wither achievement is wired (`achievements-live.js`). Wither sounds (spawn, shoot, idle) exist; hurt and death use the base set.
- Batch 17 (round 3), `tools/probes/WitherRound3Probe.java` + `tools/verify-wither-round3.mjs` write `wither-targets-reference.json` (8 targeting scenarios x 100 to 220 ticks, 3 particle runs x 30 ticks) and `wither-model-reference.json` (6 ModelRenderers, 6 poses); `src/wither-targets.test.js` replays both.
  - Target selection is now probed: the unchanged `xc.bn()` runs after the invulnerable phase (AI task lists emptied so `super.bn` draws nothing) with the genuine `World.getEntitiesWithinAABB` over real `Chunk` objects, the genuine selector `xd` and the genuine `canEntityBeSeen` ray (a wall plane). Scenarios: cows/pigs/zombie/survival and creative players across chunk and 16-high slice boundaries on Easy/Normal/Hard, a wall, creative-only (the search ends with no target), kills, and the 20/8/20 box edges. The port (`src/wither-targeting.js`, extracted from `witherAITick`; it already matched) replays every entity Random draw, head target, `bt`/`bu` timer and skull spawn position exactly. The pool is box overlap, the undead selector, then the chunk x / chunk z / slice / insertion order.
  - Head particles are probed (`xc.e()` on a client-flagged world, entity and world Random both logged): per head three gaussians (0.3) for the smoke puff, then in the armoured phase one world `nextInt(4)` and on 0 a `mobSpell` with three more gaussians, then while Invul > 0 three `mobSpell` (gaussian, `nextFloat * 3.3`, gaussian). Names, draw order, motion values (0.7, 0.7, 0.5 / 0.9) matched the earlier bytecode reading. The replay found one real error: the head positions used `Math.sin/cos`; the native code goes through MathHelper's sine table (differences up to about 5e-5 blocks, which also shifts skull spawns). `headPosition` now uses `mhSin/mhCos`.
  - ModelWither: boxes, rotation points, texture offsets and the pose matrices (spine/tail nod, tail point, centre head, side heads) are probed through the stub GL11 and replayed (`witherPose`, `partMatrix`). Fixes in the renderer: the angle constant is 57.295776 (was 1/57.3); the side heads use the entity's tracked angles directly (`xc.a(i)`, `xc.c(i)`: not interpolated by the partial tick and not wrapped); the growth scale uses the partial tick (`2 - (Invul - partial) / 220 * 0.5`); the armour pass shows whenever health is at most 150, including during the spawn (it was hidden while Invul > 0); the pass re-draws the same ModelWither (RenderWither passes `mainModel`), translated (0, -0.01, 0) and scaled 1.1 about the model origin, not a copy grown by 0.5 per side. Still bytecode-read (bpc, not run): the armour pass texture scroll, blend and colour (they need GL state the stub does not record), the growth scale and the texture flashing.
- Skull model orientation is approximate.
- Not browser-checked in this track (no dev server was started).
