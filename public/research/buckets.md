# Buckets, lakes and springs (1.7.10)

Code: `src/buckets.js` (ray trace, pickup, placement, milk), `src/lakes.js` (lakes and springs), items, recipe and fuel in `src/survival.js`, the right-click and drink wiring in `src/main.js`, the Miscellaneous tab in `src/creative.js`, `generate()` in `src/world.js`. Tests: `src/buckets.test.js`.

Read with javap from the client JAR (`tools/research/minecraft-1.7.10.jar`, names from `joined-1.7.10.srg`): `abo` ItemBucket, `adi` ItemBucketMilk, `adb` Item (`adb.a(ahb, yz, Z)` getMovingObjectPositionFromPlayer), `ahb` World (`ahb.a(azw, azw, Z, Z, Z)` rayTraceBlocks), `azu` MovingObjectPosition, `alw` BlockLiquid, `wh` EntityCow (`wh.a(yz)` interact), `yz` EntityPlayer, `apg` TileEntityFurnace, `afe` CraftingManager, `aqz` ChunkProviderGenerate (`aqz.a(ahv, II)V` populate), `arx` WorldGenLakes, `asm` WorldGenLiquids, `aia` BiomeDecorator.

## Evidence

- Fuel and container item: `node tools/verify-inventory.mjs` runs the unchanged `apg` burn-time lookup; `public/research/inventory-reference.json` now includes lava bucket (Minecraft id 327) with burn time 20000, and bucket, water bucket and milk bucket with burn time 0 and no smelting result. `apg` returns the fuel's container item (`getContainerItem`) when the last fuel in the stack is used up.
- Everything else is ported from the bytecode and covered by unit tests of this port only. No probe runs the real `abo`, `adi` or `wh` classes, and the ray trace and the lake generator are not diffed against the JAR. `JRandom` in `lakes.js` is checked against known `java.util.Random` outputs (seed 42 and seed 0).

## Rules ported

- Items (this build's ids 340 bucket, 341 water, 342 lava, 343 milk; Minecraft 325, 326, 327, 335): stack size 16 for the empty bucket and 1 for the others (`Items` registration in `ade`). Icons `bucket_empty`, `bucket_water`, `bucket_lava`, `bucket_milk`. Creative Miscellaneous order: bucket, water bucket, lava bucket, ..., milk bucket.
- Recipe (`afe`): three iron ingots, `"# #"` and `" # "`, one bucket.
- `adb.a(ahb, yz, Z)`: the ray starts at the eye and ends 5.0 blocks along the view. An empty bucket traces with `stopOnLiquid = true`, `ignoreBlocksWithoutBoundingBoxes = false`; a filled one with `false` and `true`. `World.rayTraceBlocks` is ported as the cell walk (start cell, then at most 200 more) with `Block.collisionRayTrace` per cell. `Block.canCollideCheck` (`BlockLiquid`): a liquid is only hit when stopping on liquid and the metadata is 0, so flowing water and lava are looked through and only sources are picked up.
- `abo.a(add, ahb, yz)` empty bucket: the hit block must be water or lava source (metadata 0); it is set to air (flag 3) and the stack becomes the filled bucket through `func_150910_a`: a creative player keeps the stack; a lone bucket is replaced by the filled one; otherwise one bucket is used up and the filled bucket goes into the inventory (`addItemStackToInventory`) or is thrown (`dropPlayerItemWithRandomChoice`, `hooks.drop`).
- `abo.a(add, ahb, yz)` filled bucket: the hit side moves the target to the adjacent cell (`side` 0 down to 5 east). `abo.a(ahb, III)Z` (tryPlaceContainedLiquid): the cell must be air or not solid (liquids, plants, torches); a non-air non-liquid block is broken with its drops first; then the liquid is set with metadata 0 and flag 3, so the fluid simulation, lava hardening and neighbour updates run. A creative player keeps the filled bucket; everyone else gets an empty one. Nothing is played in the Overworld (the fizz and smoke are only for water when `WorldProvider.hasNoSky`).
- `wh.a(yz)` (EntityCow.interact): a non-creative player holding an empty bucket gets a milk bucket (replace a lone bucket, otherwise exchange one). No sound, no age check, so calves can be milked.
- `adi` ItemBucketMilk: use duration 32 ticks, action `drink`, `random.drink` (imported as `public/audio/1.7.10/random/drink.ogg`, volume 0.5, pitch `r * 0.1 + 0.9` every fourth tick of the last 25, as `EntityPlayer.updateItemUse`). `onEaten`: one is used up unless creative, the stack becomes an empty bucket; `clearActivePotions` does nothing because there are no potion effects.
- Furnace fuel: lava bucket 20000 ticks (the furnace save clamps are raised to 20000); a used-up lava bucket leaves an empty bucket in the fuel slot.
- Lakes (`aqz.a(ahv, II)V`, `arx`): each chunk has a `java.util.Random` seeded from the world seed as in vanilla (`setSeed(seed)`, `xm`/`zm` = `nextLong() / 2 * 2 + 1`, then `cx * xm + cz * zm ^ seed`). Water lake: `nextInt(4) == 0`, x and z `nextInt(16) + 8`, y `nextInt(256)`. Lava lake: `nextInt(8) == 0`, y `nextInt(nextInt(248) + 8)`, generated when `y < 63` or `nextInt(10) == 0`. `WorldGenLakes`: scan down from y through air to `y > 5`, `y -= 4`, four to seven ellipsoids (`nextDouble` radii 3 to 9, 2 to 6, 3 to 9) marked in a 16 x 16 x 8 table, a check of the cells around the blob (liquid at the upper four layers or a non-solid cell in the lower four rejects the lake), the blob filled with air in the top four layers and the liquid below, dirt under the new roof that sees the sky turned to grass, and for lava the solid rim stoned (all of the lower four layers, half of the upper ones, `nextInt(2)`).
- Springs (`asm`, `aia` decorate): 50 water springs per chunk at y `nextInt(nextInt(248) + 8)` and 20 lava springs at y `nextInt(nextInt(nextInt(240) + 8) + 8)`. A spring is a stone cell (or air) with stone above and below and exactly three stone and one air neighbour at its level; it becomes a source block.
- Generated liquid is written as settled sources with the untracked `World.put` (flag 2): no saved edits, no neighbour notification, no fluid ticks on load (`Fluids.pending` is empty after generation). Lakes and springs only exist for `generationVersion >= 5`, which is the new-world default, so saves and worlds made at versions 1 to 4 keep their terrain (sha1 of versions 1 to 3 pinned in `buckets.test.js`).

## Missing / differences

- Finite world. Only chunks 0 to 6 on each axis are populated (a chunk is populated once its east, south and south-east neighbours exist); lake and spring positions use those chunks' `+8` offsets and the 64 high world, so springs and lake rolls above y = 63 are lost, and a lake that would not fit under the top is dropped. The lava lake `y < 63` test uses this world's sea level (16) instead, otherwise almost every lava lake would be a surface lake on terrain this low.
- The chunk random is the vanilla seed derivation, but dungeons, ores, trees and the other decorators that run between lakes and springs in a real chunk draw from it too and are not ported, so positions do not match a real 1.7.10 world (they could not: the terrain generator is this build's own). Lakes run before the tree pass here, as in populate; trees only grow on grass, so a lake takes the trees it covers.
- No biomes: no desert check on water lakes, no mycelium, no ice on cold-biome lakes (`isBlockFreezable`). The "sky sees the dirt" test is "nothing solid above in the column" rather than the sky light value. Trees and flowers are not removed by the lake pass because they come after it.
- No cave lava below y 10 (`WorldGenCaves`/ravines are not the Java generators); lava only comes from lava lakes and lava springs.
- Springs are settled sources: vanilla ticks a new spring at once so it begins to flow. Here a spring starts flowing when a neighbouring block changes (the fluid simulation wakes cells beside saved edits), not when the world is created. Lake water and lava are settled the same way.
- Plant bounds for the ray trace are approximations (tall grass `0.1..0.9` wide and `0.8` high, crops `0.25` high, torch `0.4..0.6`); everything else uses `blockBounds`.
- No potion effects for milk to clear, no `largesmoke` particles when water is poured in the Nether (there is no Nether), and the buckets have no sound in the Overworld apart from the drink.
- Browser behaviour was not exercised (the preview server serves the main checkout, and game audio is kept off the speakers); `npm test` and `npm run build` pass.
