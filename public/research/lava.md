# Lava (1.7.10)

Code: `src/fluids.js` (`Fluids` lava branches, `inLava`, `viewpointBlock`), block 42 in `src/world.js`, light in `src/lighting.js`, meshing, animation and fog in `src/scene.js`, player movement and fire in `movementTick` / `tickSurvival` (`src/main.js`) and `Survival.tick` (`src/survival.js`), mobs in `src/entities.js` (`burn`, `hazard`, the lava branch of `moveEntityWithHeading`), items in `src/item-entities.js`. Tests: `src/lava.test.js`. The target is 1.7.10; this build does not claim lava parity (see Missing).

Read with javap from the client JAR (SHA-1 `e80d9b3b...`): `akr` BlockDynamicLiquid, `ant` BlockStaticLiquid, `alw` BlockLiquid, `aji` Block, `awt` Material (`awt.h` water, `awt.i` lava), `aqo` WorldProvider (`f` hasNoSky, `g` isHellWorld), `ro` DamageSource (`ro.c` lava, `ro.b` onFire), `sa` Entity (`P` handleLavaMovement, `E` setOnFireFromLava, `C` onEntityUpdate), `sv` EntityLivingBase (`e(FF)` moveEntityWithHeading), `xk` EntityItem, `baj` ActiveRenderInfo (`a(ahb, sv, F)` getBlockAtEntityViewpoint). Vanilla ids 10 and 11 (flowing and still lava) are one block here, 42, like water at id 10: a flowing cell has a pending tick and a still cell has none; the level is the metadata (0 source, 1 to 7 flowing, 8 and up falling).

## Evidence

`node tools/verify-fluids.mjs` runs the unchanged `akr`, `ant` and `alw` classes through `tools/probes/FluidProbe.java` (same minimal `ahb` double as for water, now with a `java.util.Random` world Random, a WorldProvider with `hasNoSky` / `isHellWorld`, and a recorder for `ahb.a(DDDString, F, F)` sounds) in 13 lava scenarios, and writes `lava-reference.json`. `src/lava.test.js` replays it against `src/fluids.js`. A scenario is compared on: the grid hash after every tick (5,900 ticks), the final grid, the pending tick count, `getFlowVector` and `getFlowDirection` of every liquid cell (1,610), every `random.fizz` with its tick, cell, volume and float pitch (1,160 sounds), and the state of the world Random after the run (so every `nextInt(4)` and fizz `nextFloat` was drawn in the original order). The scenarios: lava-open, lava-two, lava-terraces, lava-maze, lava-pools, lava-water (+ b, c), lava-busy (+ b), and three Nether runs (rate 10, step 1). The 12 water scenarios and `fluids-reference.json` are unchanged and still pass. Block properties in the reference (`water [light 0, opacity 3, opaque false]`, `lava [15, 0, false]`) come from the same JAR.

Not run against the JAR: entity motion, fire, items, rendering and fog (ported from the bytecode listed above and covered only by unit tests of this port).

## Rules ported

- `akr.a` updateTick, lava branch. Level step 2 in the overworld (1 when `provider.hasNoSky`); tick rate 30 (10 when `isHellWorld`). Slow rise: `lava && level < 8 && next < 8 && next > level && nextInt(4) != 0` multiplies the tick delay by 4; the Random is only drawn when the earlier conditions hold. No infinite source (the two-sources rule is water-only). Lava over water: `setBlock(below, stone)`, fizz, return. Falling lava spreads sideways at level 1; sideways spread is `level + step` and stops at 8 or more.
- `akr.q` canFlowInto: not the same material, not lava, not blocked: water never flows into lava, lava flows into water. `akr.h` flowing into a cell: lava fizzes (`alw.m`) and does not drop what was there (water washes torches, wheat and tall grass).
- `alw.n` hardening, from `onBlockAdded` and every neighbour change: water at z-1, z+1, x-1, x+1 or y+1 (never y-1). Source lava (meta 0) becomes obsidian (22), meta 1 to 4 cobblestone (12), and the fizz always plays. Neighbour change on still lava hardens, then makes it flowing (which hardens again) and schedules a tick; on flowing lava it only hardens. The scheduled-tick key is cell plus block, so a water and a lava entry for one cell are distinct.
- `alw.m` fizz: `random.fizz`, volume 0.5, pitch `2.6 + (r1 - r2) * 0.8` in floats from two `world.s.nextFloat()` draws, at the cell centre. (The 8 `largesmoke` particles use `Math.random` and are not drawn.) Obsidian and cobblestone already existed as blocks 22 and 12.
- `aji` properties: hardness 100 (never mined: lava is not targetable, `raycast` skips it), light 15, light opacity 0, not opaque, not solid, no creative tab. `lightEmission(42) = 15`, `lightOpacity(42) = 0`; shadows treat it like water (`NOT_NORMAL`, `shadow-reference.json` gained block 42).
- Rendering: `getRenderBlockPass` is 0 for lava, so it is meshed in the opaque pass with `lava_still` (20 frames, frametime 2, ping-pong 0..19..1) and `lava_flow` (32 x 32 frames, 16 of them, frametime 3), no depth pre-pass, no blend and no biome tint (`colorMultiplier` is white). The top, flow rotation, corner heights, sides and brightness code is shared with water (`liquid(kind, ...)`); lava brightness is the brighter of the cell and the one above, so it glows.
- Fog: while `getBlockAtEntityViewpoint` is lava (the eye cell, or the one above it once the eye is `meta / 9` or more below the top, `percent(meta) - 1/9`), fog is `GL_EXP` density 2.0 and colour (0.6, 0.1, 0.0) times the fog brightness, the sky dome takes the same colour and the sunrise fan is hidden.
- `sa.P` handleLavaMovement: the box shrunk by (0.1, 0.4, 0.1) and `isMaterialInBB` (any lava cell in `floor(min)..floor(max + 1)`, no surface-height test). `sa.C` order: water puts the fire out; fire > 0 deals 1 `onFire` damage whenever the counter is a multiple of 20, then counts down; in lava, `attackEntityFrom(lava, 4)`, `setFire(15)` (the counter keeps the larger of its value and 300) and the fall distance is halved. The existing hurt-resistance windows still gate each hit (player `hurtTicks`, mobs `hurt` / `lastDamage`). Lava damage uses the armour calculation, burning does not.
- `sv.e(FF)` lava branch (only outside water, not for a flying player): `moveFlying(strafe, forward, 0.02)` whatever the ground or sprint, move, motion x 0.5 on every axis, `motionY -= 0.02`, and a 0.3 climb when blocked horizontally and `isOffsetPositionInLiquid(motionX, motionY + 0.6 - dy, motionZ)` is clear of blocks and any liquid. Jumping in lava adds 0.04 (no ground jump). Mobs also run `EntityAISwimming` in lava (80 % jump) and burning animals keep fleeing (`EntityAIPanic`).
- `xk` EntityItem: health 5, `attackEntityFrom` subtracts. In `onUpdate`, when the item changes cell or every 25th tick and its cell is lava: `motionY = 0.2`, x and z `(r1 - r2) * 0.2`, `random.fizz` volume 0.4, pitch `2 + 0.4 r`. Lava damage and fire go through the same `sa.C` code, so an item in lava is at health 1 after the first tick and dies on the second.

## Missing / differences

- No way to get lava in play: it is not in the creative tabs (it is in none in 1.7.10), there is no bucket, no world generation (cave lava lakes, the Nether) and no `largesmoke` particles. Lava can only come from edited saves or code.
- Static lava does not set fire to neighbouring blocks (`ant.a` / `ant.func_149674_a` fire spread) because there is no fire block yet. Lava also does not ignite flammable blocks it flows into.
- No first-person fire overlay (the `fire_layer_0/1` textures are imported but unused); mobs reuse the existing burning tint (`burn`) and the player has none. No `liquid.lava` / `liquid.lavapop` ambient sounds (these come from the block's random display tick, which this build does not run); `random.fizz` was already imported.
- Burning mobs and the player do not make the `fire.fire` hiss, and zombie sunlight still uses the older 1-damage-per-second shortcut rather than `setFire(8)`.
- Player movement is this build's simplified model (velocity then drag), with the lava constants of `sv.e(FF)` applied to it; mobs use the ported `moveEntityWithHeading` and items the ported `EntityItem` motion.
- Entities sink by their box in the same blocks as in water, but water's own eye test in `scene.js` stays the older `fraction < 8/9` shortcut; only the lava fog uses `getBlockAtEntityViewpoint` as in the bytecode (for a source cell the bytecode keeps the eye in the liquid until it leaves the cell, which differs from the water shortcut).
- Nether rules (rate 10, step 1) are ported and verified but nothing selects them yet: `Fluids` takes a `nether` flag that `main.js` never sets.
- Browser rendering of lava was not exercised (no preview run); `npm test` and `npm run build` pass.
