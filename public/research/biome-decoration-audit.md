# Biome decoration audit (batch 16)

Evidence that `BiomeDecorator.decorate` / `BiomeGenBase.decorate` and the biome overrides, as ported in `src/biome-decorator.js` and `src/world-gen*.js`, replay the unchanged 1.7.10 `ChunkProviderGenerate.populate` on every naturally occurring biome. It does not claim full parity.

## What is probed

- `tools/find-biome-audit-regions.mjs` scans seeds with the ported layers and picks a 6x6-chunk region for each natural slot (plus extra regions for mutated and variant slots). Result: `tools/probes/biome-audit-regions.json`, 69 regions, 56 slots.
- `tools/probes/PopulateChainProbe.java` in audit mode (`-Daudit=<file>`) builds native terrain, caves and ravines for each region, populates the inner 2x2 chunks with the unchanged native populate (map features off), then removes tile entities. Reference: `biome-audit-reference.json` (generated by `tools/verify-biome-audit.mjs`).
- `src/biome-audit.test.js` replays each region with the JS port and compares terrain input hash, call-order and write-order SHA-1s, state hash, pending ticks, tile entities, spawned animals and the next Random long. It also asserts per-biome signature features (for example emerald and silverfish in Extreme Hills, lily pad, vine, clay and reeds in Swamp, melon in Jungle, packed ice in Ice Plains Spikes, double plants in Sunflower Plains, cactus and dead bush in Mesa).

## Port bug found and fixed

Mutated biomes (`BiomeGenMutated`) delegate `getBiomeClass` and `isEqualTo` to their base biome. The port compared slots, so ShoreLayer treated Jungle M (149) as incompatible with jungle neighbours and produced Jungle Edge where native produces Beach. `src/java-biomes.js` now carries `biomeClass` and `src/biome-layers.js` uses it. This changes the biome map near mutated jungle shores. The generation version was not bumped; that is a lead decision.

## Lily pad

`RenderBlocks.renderBlockLilyPad` emits two flat quads (top and underside) with colour 0x208030 for every colour method, so the tint was already correct. The mesher now skips the side faces. Native rotates UVs by a position hash; that is not ported.

## Not covered

- Slots 10, 20 and 151 do not occur as natural biomes in the scanned seeds; they are covered only by earlier cycled-slot records. Slots 8 and 9 are not Overworld population.
- Desert wells (1/1000 per chunk) did not fire in the audit; they and the jungle melon feature rest on feature-level override records.
- Mossy cobblestone in taigas cannot be told from dungeon mossy cobblestone by id alone.
- Block and sky light in the replay are fixtures, not a light engine.
- Structures and map features are off. Worldgen animals are compared as entity records only.
- Everything above was probed against the native jar; no part is bytecode-read only.
