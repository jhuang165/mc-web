# Biome decoration audit (batches 16 and 17)

Evidence that `BiomeDecorator.decorate` / `BiomeGenBase.decorate` and the biome overrides, as ported in `src/biome-decorator.js` and `src/world-gen*.js`, replay the unchanged 1.7.10 `ChunkProviderGenerate.populate` on every naturally occurring biome. It does not claim full parity.

## What is probed

- `tools/find-biome-audit-regions.mjs` scans seeds with the ported layers and picks a 6x6-chunk region for each natural slot (plus extra regions for mutated and variant slots). Result: `tools/probes/biome-audit-regions.json`, 69 natural regions over 56 slots plus ten forced regions (batch 17, below): 79 regions, 59 slots. Each region draws its world Random and light base from its own stream (seeded by seed and origin), so a region can be added or scanned alone.
- `tools/probes/PopulateChainProbe.java` in audit mode (`-Daudit=<file>`) builds native terrain, caves and ravines for each region, populates the inner 2x2 chunks with the unchanged native populate (map features off), then removes tile entities. Reference: `biome-audit-reference.json` (generated by `tools/verify-biome-audit.mjs`).
- `src/biome-audit.test.js` replays each region with the JS port and compares terrain input hash, call-order and write-order SHA-1s, state hash, pending ticks, tile entities, spawned animals and the next Random long. It also asserts per-biome signature features (for example emerald and silverfish in Extreme Hills, lily pad, vine, clay and reeds in Swamp, melon in Jungle, packed ice in Ice Plains Spikes, double plants in Sunflower Plains, cactus and dead bush in Mesa).

## Batch 17: forced regions, chunk-level wells and melons, structures on

- Forced single-biome regions (a fifth region field of 1): the probe swaps the world's chunk manager for the unchanged `WorldChunkManagerHell` (`aie`) with the slot's biome, for terrain, caves, ravines and populate alike; `src/forced-biome-manager.js` is the research-only JS twin (used by `src/biome-audit.test.js`, selected by no generation version). It covers the three slots that no scanned seed produced naturally: 10 FrozenOcean (clay and ice written), 20 Extreme Hills Edge (emerald, silverfish, logs) and 151 JungleEdge M (logs, vines, cocoa), two seeds each. Terrain under a forced biome is synthetic (the same biome everywhere, no river or shore), so these records pin populate, not the natural look of those slots. The slot names were checked against the probe: 140 is Ice Plains Spikes (already natural), 151 is JungleEdge M.
- Desert wells and jungle melons: each record now carries the native count of `stone_slab:1` writes (only `WorldGenDesertWells` places it in these populates) and of melon blocks; the replay asserts the JS counts equal them. A native scan (`AUDIT_SCAN=1 AUDIT_REGIONS=<file> node tools/verify-biome-audit.mjs`, 2,800 forced Desert regions) found chunks where the 1/1000 well draw fires and the site accepts it (12 slab writes per well); two are in the audit (seeds 2091 and 3271). Melons now fire at chunk level in slots 21 (forced seed 136, 9 melons), 22 (seed 120) and 23 (natural, 9 melons). Desert Hills (17) did not produce a well in 500 forced regions; Desert M (130) is not scanned. The feature-level override records are no longer the only evidence for these two.
- Structures on: `tools/probes/StructureAreaProbe.java` (unchanged from batch 14) run by `tools/verify-structure-area.mjs` with `STRUCTURE_AREAS_FILE=tools/probes/biome-audit-structure-areas.json STRUCTURE_REFERENCE=biome-audit-structures-reference.json` gives `biome-audit-structures-reference.json`: 14 areas (3x3 or 4x4 chunks, 154 populated chunks, 318,535 writes) of the unchanged `ChunkProviderGenerate` with all four map features: plains and savanna villages, two swamp huts, a desert pyramid, mineshafts under hills, cold taiga, roofed forest and ocean, and strongholds under savanna M, hills, roofed forest and birch hills. Every chunk is provided (terrain, caves, ravines, four range-8 start searches) and then populated; `src/biome-audit-structures.test.js` replays them through `StructurePopulationArea` and asserts structure starts, ordered call and write hashes, the provider Random and World.rand after each chunk (so structure populate order, village lake suppression and the Random handed to lakes, dungeons and the biome's decorate), final chunk hashes, tile entities, entities, carts, ticks and drops. Areas were picked with `StructurePopulation` search over seeds 1-30; a stronghold area in Extreme Hills M was dropped because its door/button path hits an unported stone-button power read (`structure redstone power from block 77`), and jungle temples stay in `structure-area.md`.
- Lily pad UV rotation is ported (below).

## Port bug found and fixed

Mutated biomes (`BiomeGenMutated`) delegate `getBiomeClass` and `isEqualTo` to their base biome. The port compared slots, so ShoreLayer treated Jungle M (149) as incompatible with jungle neighbours and produced Jungle Edge where native produces Beach. `src/java-biomes.js` now carries `biomeClass` and `src/biome-layers.js` uses it. This changes the biome map near mutated biomes (for example seed 671 at x=-224, z=16 is Jungle Hills natively and Jungle Edge under the old rule). The lead gated it behind streamed generation 16: new streamed worlds and level.dat imports with MapFeatures use the native rule; finite worlds (generation 14) and saved streamed worlds below 16 pass `legacyMutated` (`java-world.js` `biomeLayerOptions`) so their unvisited terrain does not shift. Tools and tests that build the layers without a version get the native rule. Tested in `src/biome-generation-gate.test.js`.

## Lily pad

`RenderBlocks.renderBlockLilyPad` emits two flat quads (top and underside) with colour 0x208030 for every colour method, so the tint was already correct. The mesher now skips the side faces. Batch 17 ports the position hash: `l = (int)(x * 3129871) ^ (long)z * 116129781L ^ y; l = l * l * 42317861L + l * 11L; rotation = (l >> 16) & 3`, and the four quad corners with their texture corners as `blm.o` orders them (`src/lily-pad.js`, applied to the top and underside quads in `scene.js` `cube()`). `src/lily-pad.test.js` pins the hash against 16 positions computed with Java `int`/`long` arithmetic from the bytecode expression and the corner table for all four rotations. Native shades the underside at half the top colour (`(colour & 0xFEFEFE) >> 1`); that is not ported here. The renderer itself was not run in a browser.

## Not covered

- Slots 10, 20 and 151 are covered by forced single-biome regions only (synthetic terrain), not by natural ones. Slots 8 and 9 are not Overworld population.
- Desert Hills and Desert M wells, and jungle temples with structures on (known decoration divergence in `structure-area.md`), are not covered at chunk level.
- Mossy cobblestone in taigas cannot be told from dungeon mossy cobblestone by id alone.
- Block and sky light in the replay are fixtures, not a light engine.
- The 79 per-biome regions run with map features off; structures are covered by the 14 separate areas above, not by every slot. Worldgen animals are compared as entity records only.
- Everything above was probed against the native jar; no part is bytecode-read only.
