All **57 numeric EntityList registrations**, read from its complete map rather than inferred from spawn eggs. Native class names are obfuscated names from the pinned original JAR. Abstract entries are registrations, not independently spawnable creatures. Spawn-egg presence is native evidence only; browser spawn eggs remain absent.

Gate **E** depends on WORLD-01/SIM-01 plus player/combat, mob/spawning, transport, progression or dimensions as applicable. Each row requires native identity and variant metadata, dimensions, attributes, spawn/despawn/caps, movement/collision/fluids, AI/targets/interaction/equipment, damage/immunity/knockback/attribution, drops/rewards, render/animation/sound, save/unload/reload and cross-system acceptance cases. Partial links state current evidence and limitations; registry extraction verifies enumeration only. Unmapped needs review before declaring Missing.

EntityList is not the complete feature roster: players, weather lightning, thrown eggs and fishing hooks need separate non-registration cases; rider/jockey combinations, baby/tamed/equipment/difficulty states, wither skeletons, zombie villagers, charged creepers, horse variants, bosses and multipart entities require semantic subcases. Registration coverage does not close that work.
