# Blaze, Ghast and their fireballs (Minecraft Java 1.7.10)

Original classes: Blaze `xx`, Ghast `yd` (extends `sr`/EntityFlying), EntityFireball `ze`, large `zg`, small `zi`, ModelBlaze `bhd`, RenderBlaze `bnd`, ModelGhast `bhl`, RenderGhast `bnt`, dispenser fire charge `kz`, RenderGlobal level events `bma`.

## Evidence

- Probe `tools/probes/BlazeGhastProbe.java` drives the unchanged client classes; `tools/verify-blaze-ghast.mjs` writes `public/research/blaze-ghast-reference.json` (needs `MC_RESEARCH` and a JDK). Record counts: facts 1, xp 1, blazeDrops 320, ghastDrops 160, blazeStart 6, blazeVolley 1560, blazeMelee 1, blazeUpdate 440, blazeFall 2, ghastStart 2, ghastAi 800, fireballStart 4, fireballFlight 160, fireballExplicit 1, largeImpact 3, smallImpact 2, smallProps 1, fireballHurt 1, ghastHurt 2, nbt 1.
- `src/blaze-ghast.test.js` replays those rows against `src/blaze-ghast.js`; `src/blaze-ghast-world.test.js` checks the engine wiring.
- Batch 39 (legacyai): `LegacyAiProbe.java` runs the unchanged `xx` (blaze) and `yd` (ghast) classes whole; `src/legacy-ai.test.js` replays 12 blaze/ghast/mixed records exactly against `public/research/legacy-ai-reference.json` (positions, motion, yaw, hover and volley fields, entityAge, jumping, path presence, Random draws, sounds, level events 1007-1009, fireball launch vectors and seeds, the player's hits). The blaze now runs EntityCreature's legacy action state (`blazeThink` over `creatureActionState` in `silverfish.js`, `hasAttacked` skips the re-path and wander and strafes) instead of a task list.
- Bytecode-read, not probed: `td.bq` (EntityCreature.updateEntityActionState: hasAttacked, strafing, isJumping persistence, `getPathEntityToEntity(..., true, false, false, true)`), `xx.e` (draw order: wet damage, hover countdown, crackle roll, 0.6 damping, six smoke doubles), `sv.attackEntityFrom` (entityAge = 0).
- Bytecode-derived, not probed: dispenser fire charge, models, renderers, sounds and level events, the blaze client crackle and smoke, the small fireball block case.

## Behaviour

- Blaze: hover and fly, fire immune, 1 damage per tick while wet, 3-shot small-fireball volley (60 tick wind-up, 6 tick gaps, 100 tick rest), melee 6, rods with looting (player hit only), 10 XP, rotating-rod model, fullbright, sounds mob.blaze.*.
- Ghast: flying wander and target AI, charge-up (1007 warning, face texture switches, 1008 fire), large fireball with `ExplosionPower` NBT (default 1), tears and gunpowder, 5 XP, a player-deflected fireball kills it.
- Small fireball: 5 damage plus 5 s of fire, lights the cell beside a hit block, no mobGriefing check. Large fireball: 6 damage then a flaming explosion, smoking follows mobGriefing; a player punch deflects it.
- Dispenser: a fire charge launches a small fireball. Spawn eggs 61 and 56 and /summon Blaze, /summon Ghast work; monster-spawner ids Blaze and Ghast are accepted. Region export and import round trip both mobs.

## Gaps

- Natural spawning: ghasts from BiomeGenHell's list and blazes from the fortress list through the SpawnerAnimals port ([natural spawning](natural-spawning.md)); the Nether flow is not covered by a seeded replay. No achievements.
- Fireballs are not saved and there is no /summon Fireball; fireball-versus-fireball collisions are not implemented.
- Ghast ground friction is approximated (0.546); a blaze or ghast fireball in flight and its impact on the player are not native evidence (the probe never ticks the spawned fireballs, only the launches are replayed). The blaze only targets the player (an attacking mob does not become its target).
- Models, renderers, crackle and smoke, level-event sounds and the small-fireball block case were not run against the original.
- The ghast render scale is a constant 4.5, assuming the client copy's attack counters never change.
- Audio files are not imported (the lead imports them); the media importer regex only gained `blaze|ghast`. Missing sounds fail silently.
- Hover offsets after the first gaussian pair are not replayed in the probe (shared Entity.rand).
- Not checked in a browser. Full parity is not claimed.

## Batch 40: fireball flight and impact, the EntityMob target rule

- `public/research/fireballs-reference.json` (tools/probes/FireballProbe.java via tools/verify-fireballs.mjs, replayed by `src/fireballs-native.test.js` / `src/fireballs-replay.js`): 27 records of the unchanged `zg`, `zi` and `ze.h` on the LegacyAi double. They cover acceleration and the 0.95 / 0.8 drag, `ticksInAir` (the 25 tick shooter grace, a punch does not reset it), block and entity intercepts (0.3 grow, nearest hit, the `d < best || best == 0` quirk), a ball hitting another large ball, the player's deflecting punch (small balls refuse), explosion power and `flaming`/`smoking` with mobGriefing on and off, the small ball's fire cell (no rule check) and `setFire(5)` unless immune, and knockback of the player double.
- Port fixes found by the replay: the dispenser form `(x, y, z, ax, ay, az)` normalises its aim like the shooter form; the first update keeps the constructor's box; water is tested before the sweep; an impact does not stop the update; large balls are collision targets; a cake's ray box is its shaped box.
- `EntityMob.attackEntityFrom` (`yg.a`): the attacker of a counted hit becomes `entityToAttack` for the blaze, silverfish, giant, spider and pigman (a mob or the player); refused hits and the victim itself change nothing; a shooterless ball is its own source entity (knocks back, becomes a dead target that the next `td.bq` clears). Blazes now shoot and melee a mob target.
- Bytecode-read only: `World.blockExists` (y outside 0 to 255 or an unloaded chunk removes the ball), the `inGround` path, `ye`/`yk` writing no `bc` (isJumping persists in the giant and silverfish).
- The blaze crackle (`fire.fire`) is the client half: a `clientOnly` client sound at the blaze; the server draws are unchanged.

## Batch 42: one entity list order, ghast balls at mobs, snowballs at a blaze

- `public/research/fireballs-b42-reference.json` (17 records; `tools/probes/FireballProbe.java`, `FB_PREFIX=fb42-`, `tools/verify-fireballs.mjs`; replayed by `src/fireballs-b42.test.js`): the same double, with a `unified` walk of ONE list in insertion order as `World.updateEntities` does (executed). A ball launched before a mob joined the list updates before it (it sweeps the mob's last position, and a ball that kills a mob lands before the mob's own update); a ball a mob launches joins the end and updates in the same pass. Ops: `join k` (mob k joins at that tick), `idle k` (never joins, a damage source only), `health k h`, `snow v i`.
- Port: `EntityWorld.orderStamp` and `withBalls` stamp each mob and ball when first seen (balls at launch, mobs at the next launch or tick, in list order) and `tickMobs` walks them merged by stamp; `Fireballs.tickOne` runs one ball, `reap` drops the dead. Batch 41 had ticked the balls after all mobs; that order fails eight of the nine order records (the mob-first control passes either way) (the test patches it back to prove it).
- What still has its own list and tick outside the walk: arrows (`arrows.list`, main.js; the `lateEntity` hook stands in for them in replays), thrown potions, golem snowballs, wither skulls (all tick before the mobs in `tickMobs`), items, XP orbs, falling blocks, TNT, minecarts, boats, hanging entities. They keep their own order against the mobs and against the balls; merging them would need one list for the whole world, which is a rewrite. Restored balls (save load) take the stamp after the mobs known at the first tick.
- Ghast balls at mobs (executed, mobs ticking): zombie, skeleton, blaze, pigman, spider, silverfish and an iron golem alone, large and small. The blaze, pigman and silverfish take the ghast as entityToAttack, as the batch 40 rule says; the port already matched. Nothing in 1.7.10 lets a mob punch a ball (only a player's `attackEntityFrom` deflects it).
- Ghast target rule (executed, slot 9 of the ghast in the rows is `EntityGhast.targetedEntity`): hurt by a zombie, a skeleton's arrow or a snow golem's snowball, the ghast keeps targeting only the player (`updateEntityActionState` takes `getClosestVulnerablePlayerToEntity(this, 100)`). The port matched.
- Snowball at a blaze: `EntitySnowball.onImpact` (`zj.a`, executed through reflection) does 3 damage to an EntityBlaze and 0 to others, with `causeThrownDamage(ball, thrower)`; a blaze hit by a golem's ball targets the golem. The port did 0 and no source entity: `thrownHit` now does 3 for a blaze, and a mob thrower is the source (player flag off). Player snowballs reach it through `main.js` `projectileHit(e, ball)`. Arrows hurting a blaze in flight are not recorded: the damage path (`hurt` kind `a<i>` in fireballs-b41-reference.json, a skeleton as the source entity) is executed, the flying arrow is the port's `bow.js` and was not replayed against this double.
- The blaze crackle is delivered at the client entity's position plus 0.5 on each axis (`offset: 0.5` on the sound, applied in `drainClientSounds`; bytecode-read `xx.e`: `playSound(posX + 0.5, posY + 0.5, posZ + 0.5, "fire.fire", ...)`).
- Open: an idle wandering legacy mob (spider, pigman, blaze) turns differently from native on some seeds (the batch 41 zombie gap, seeds 4221 and 4225 here, so the records use seeds where it does not occur); a silverfish dies at tick 0 in the port on seeds 4213, 4214, 4222 and 4226 where native keeps its 8 health (cause not found); an iron golem beside a blaze attacks it natively on tick 9 where the port's golem does not (seed 4210 with the golem in the lane row; the golem record alone matches).

## Batch 43: items, orbs, arrows, thrown entities, TNT and falling blocks join the list

- `public/research/entity-list-reference.json` (15 records; `tools/probes/EntityListProbe.java`, `tools/verify-entity-list.mjs`; replayed by `src/entity-list.test.js` through `src/entity-list-replay.js`): the unchanged `EntityItem`, `EntityXPOrb`, `EntityTNTPrimed`, `EntityArrow` and an `EntityCow` (AI task list off) walked as `World.updateEntities` walks `loadedEntityList` (executed): index loop, `ticksExisted++` then `onUpdate`, an entity spawned in the pass joins the end and updates in it, dead ones leave after the pass. Each scenario runs in both list orders. Results: two equal stacks that merge in one tick leave the survivor the other way round (items-equal-ab/ba; three stacks of 20, 20 and 30 end as 40 and 30 or as 50 and 20); a later-joining item updates after the earlier one; the player (a shell with `onCollideWithPlayer` and `xpCooldown`) takes the first orb of the list, 7 or 3; a blast pushes a TNT updated after it this tick and one updated before it a tick later; a TNT blast kills an item listed after it before its own update, and moves an item listed before it first; an arrow reaches a sliding cow before or after the cow moves (the cow is at x 2.29 or 1.96 after the hit).
- Port: `mobs.walks` (`entityWalkers` in `src/entity-simulation.js`, set in `main.js`) names six kinds, `item`, `arrow`, `thrown`, `tnt`, `orb`, `falling`, each with its live list, a per-entity `tick` and a `reap`. `EntityWorld.joined(e, kind)` stamps a spawn the way `orderStamp` stamps a ball (`ItemEntities.spawn`, `Arrows.shootPlayer`/`skeletonShoot`, `Projectiles.spawnThrowable`, `ExperienceOrbs.spawn`, `FallingBlocks.fall` and `watchJoin` on the primed-TNT array call it); an entity with no stamp (a saved one, a spawn site that does not report) is placed after the mobs known so far, as batch 42 placed restored balls. `withBalls` merges every kind and the balls with the mobs by stamp, `tickMobs` dispatches each through `walkOf`, and a spawn during the pass is appended to the pass. After the walk `finishEntitySimulation` runs the rest of the old non-living segment (item pickup, `tickEffects`, projectile events, mob events, arrow pickup), then the object tracker. Without `walks` (tests, the away run) `tickEntitySimulation` keeps its old fixed order.
- Measured (Node, `artifacts/b43-entitylist/bench.mjs`, 300 items, 150 orbs, 60 arrows, 30 cows, load average 14 to 21 on the shared machine): 2.34 to 2.64 ms per tick with the old order (release 0.71.0 source), 2.40 to 2.58 with the walk; the merge itself is about 0.03 ms. Muted headless `tps` check (`artifacts/b43-entitylist/tps.mjs`, port 5438): 160 ticks in 8 s in the Overworld and in the Nether.
- Not done: minecarts and boats (own lists and ticks), hanging entities, the away-run loop (`away-entities.js` ticks mobs, orbs, rockets, items, thrown entities and arrows per kind from saved records, with no stamps), potions, golem snowballs and skulls (they tick in the `tickMobs` prelude, before the mobs), the player pickup order of items (`collectItems` keeps the array order, which equals the list order among items), and an arrow-hits-cow replay (the arrow-cow records are executed but only checked for the order effect, the port has no cow with its AI off).
