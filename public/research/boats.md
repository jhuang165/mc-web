# Boats (EntityBoat, entity id 41, item 333)

Target: Minecraft Java 1.7.10. Not full parity; see "Remaining differences".

## Evidence

`tools/probes/BoatProbe.java` (run by `tools/verify-boats.mjs`) drives the unchanged client classes `xi` (EntityBoat), `abj` (ItemBoat) and `sa` (Entity) on a map-backed world, with `setBlock`, `setBlockToAir`, `func_147480_a` recorded and `spawnParticle` logged. Output: `public/research/boat-reference.json`, 62 records: 19 travel scenarios (buoyancy, rider input, speed cap, snow and lily pad clearing, crash and fall breaks, shore, ice, lava, splash particles), 6 hit/break sequences, 1 mount geometry record, 36 ItemBoat placement records, plus sha256 digests of the three classes. `src/boats.test.js` replays them (tolerance 1e-7 on double state; float state exact).

## Implemented

- Item id 495 (original 333): 5 planks in a U, Creative Transportation tab and search order after the saddle, original `items/boat.png` icon.
- ItemBoat: 5-block trace that stops on source water, works on land, snow layer cell one lower, yaw snapped to the player's axis, refused when the box shrunk by 0.1 meets blocks. Taken from the stack except in Creative. ItemBoat has no `onItemUse`, so a right click on an activated block (chest, door, ...) opens that block and only `onItemRightClick` places a boat (`player-edit-simulation.js`).
- EntityBoat.onUpdate: five-slice buoyancy, rider forward/strafe input along the rider yaw, speed multiplier limits 0.07 to 0.35, drag, collision break above speed 0.2 (3 planks and 2 sticks), fall above 3 breaks (3 planks and 2 sticks), snow layers and lily pads cleared, hit damage and wobble (renders as RenderBoat's roll), player hit break (drops the boat; Creative breaks without a drop), splash and bubble particles with the splash sound, 0.05 pushes between a boat and players or mobs.
- ModelBoat (5 boxes) with `entity/boat.png`, RenderBoat transform (`src/boat-renderer.js`).
- Riding: right click mounts (one rider), Shift dismounts to the native position, F5 seated pose, rider camera through the existing riding path. Strafe sign: the browser's D is +1, native moveStrafing is positive to the left.
- Persistence: `boats` array in saves, chunk runtime storage and exports (written only when a boat exists; old saves load with none). The mounted state is restored through `restorePlayerVehicle`.
- Standing on and walking into boats (batch 14): `EntityBoat.getBoundingBox` (xi.J) returns its box, so `World.getCollidingBoundingBoxes` hands it to every mover. The player and all mobs that use `moveEntity` stand on it, are blocked by its sides and step onto it with their step height (`src/entity-collision.js`, installed into `entities.js` from `main.js`; the boat the mover rides is skipped). `EntityMinecart.getBoundingBox` (xl.J) returns null, so players and mobs walk through carts (the cart's own `getCollisionBox` only blocks the cart). 9 native `collide` records (stand, side hit on both axes, step, raised boat, rise from below, edge, two cart pass-throughs) are replayed to 1e-7 in `boats.test.js`.
- ItemBoat's line of sight (batch 14): after the trace hits, every collidable entity in the player's box swept 5 along the look vector and grown by 1 aborts the placement when its box grown by 0.1 contains the **mutated start vector** of `rayTraceBlocks` (the entry point of the last cell visited, not the eye). `buckets.js rayTraceBlocks` exposes that vector. 13 native `sight` records (none, eye inside a boat, beside, on the target, hit-entry boats including Creative, slanted traces) are replayed. The overlap test on the 0.1-shrunk box then uses every entity box (player, mobs, boats, carts).
- Mob passengers (batch 14, `src/vehicle-seat.js`, `src/vehicle-riders.js`): a mob mounts a boat through `Entity.mountEntity`, which `/summon Zombie ~ ~ ~ {Riding:{id:Boat}}` performs. It sits at `x + cos(yaw) * 0.4, posY - 0.3 + getYOffset, z + sin(yaw) * 0.4` (skeletons 0.5 lower), is carried with the boat, turns with it like other riders and falls free when the boat dies; a boat with a mob on it refuses a player. Boats never pick up mobs by themselves. The save keeps `riding: "boat-<id>"` on the mob and an `id` on the boat.
- The 0.5 boat shadow (`RenderBoat.shadowSize`, `shadowOpaque` 1) is drawn by the existing shadow system; `EntityBoat.getShadowSize` is 0, so the cast starts at the boat's `posY`.

## Remaining differences

- A mob passenger is kept only through `/summon` with a `Riding` tag (and the browser save); region export writes no boats, so a boat passenger exports as a loose mob, and Java region import of a `Riding` boat is not read. A mob riding a boat leaves it when the boat dies and does not take the boat's landing fall damage. Streamed-out chunks do not keep a mob and its boat linked (the `riding` id is only resolved when a world is loaded).
- Boat-pair pushes, lava burning, the void kill and the rider position right after a break are implemented but not covered by native records.
