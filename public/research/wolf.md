## Batch 43: wolf AI draws and whole-record replay (wanderdraws)

The wolf's EntityAI* tasks draw on Entity.rand (`ai.js` gives the wolf, and the sheep with its EatGrass, the entity's own Random like the zombie, villager and chicken). Tick-0 native draws, replayed exactly: wild wolf `i1000 i200 i120 f f` (living sound, TargetNonTamed 1 in 200, Wander 1 in 120, WatchClosest and LookIdle 2 % rolls), tamed `i1000 i120 f f`, sitting `i1000 f` (Sit holds mutex 5; LookIdle's draw stays, Wander is blocked), begging `... i120 i40` (Beg draws its 40 + nextInt(40) timer and its mutex 2 blocks WatchClosest and LookIdle). All 30 b42 wolf records and the 8 b43wd- wolf records (idle wild and tamed, begging for a bone or porkchop, following the owner with teleport, a sitting follower, two wolves) are exact over their whole length.

Fixes: EntityAIBeg (`ty.a(yz)`, bytecode-read) is true for a bone only while the wolf is untamed, otherwise for a favourite meat (`wv.c`), so an untamed wolf also begs for meat; the port had bone for wild and meat for tamed only. EntityAILeapAtTarget takes `MathHelper.sqrt_double`, a float (the leap velocity differed at 1e-9). A dead target is not dropped by the wolf's own tick any more: EntityAITarget.continueExecuting ends it in the task update. A hit by a tamed wolf sets the victim's `recentlyHit` to 100 (EntityLivingBase.attackEntityFrom has the EntityWolf branch, with no attackingPlayer), so its drops roll the player-kill rare drop (`i200`) as in the records.
