# Live villager trading (Java 1.7.10)

Right-clicking an adult villager opens a Villager merchant screen. It reuses the standalone modules `src/villager-offers.js` (offer generation) and `src/villager-trading.js` (matching, uses, persistence) and adds `src/merchant-ui.js`, the live villager state in `src/villager.js`, a screen in `src/main.js` and `src/container-ui.css`. It is not full parity.

## Evidence

`tools/probes/VillagerTradeLiveProbe.java` drives the unchanged original classes EntityVillager (`yv`), ContainerMerchant (`aat`), InventoryMerchant (`aas`), SlotMerchantResult (`aau`), MerchantRecipe (`agn`) and MerchantRecipeList (`ago`). Only the player (`InventoryProbe.ProbePlayer` plus `displayGUIMerchant`, which opens the real container and then calls `getRecipes`) and the world are doubles. `tools/verify-villager-trading-live.mjs` writes `villager-trading-live-reference.json`: 60 scripted sessions (5 professions x 12 seeds, about 13,200 steps) and 4 interact refusals (adult, child, already trading, spawn egg in hand). After every step the probe records all 39 container slots, the cursor, dropped stacks, villager sounds (name, volume, float pitch), the whole offer list with uses and maxUses, timeUntilReset, needsInit, Riches, the living-sound counter, the customer flag, the Regeneration effect and health; each session ends with the next `nextInt` of the entity Random and of the shuffle Random. `src/villager-trading-live.test.js` replays the sessions through `MerchantContainer`, `villagerInteract`, `villagerUseRecipe`, `villagerHover` and `tickVillagerRestock` and compares every field and both Random streams: all identical. The sessions cover about 1,000 result pickups with the yes sound, swapped payment slots, short payments, shift-clicks, selecting out-of-range offers, closing with payment in the slots, 162 restock-generated offers, 106 max-use increases and Regeneration.

Two defects the probe found while porting: shift-click routing in ContainerMerchant is main to hotbar, hotbar to main, payment and result to player slots (not into the payment slots), and `MerchantRecipeList.a(first, second, index)` scans the list when the index is zero, negative or past the end (`findMatchingOffer` returned "no match" for the last case).

## What works

- Interact (`EntityVillager.a(yz)`): an alive, adult, idle villager without a spawn egg in hand takes the player as its customer; a trading villager stops walking (EntityAITradePlayer, EntityAILookAtTradePlayer are live tasks). The screen opens, then offers are generated on first access (`getRecipes`): `t(1)` from the entity's own Random plus `Collections.shuffle`, with the old-list-size bonus on restock.
- Container: payment slots 0 and 1, result slot 2, 27 + 9 player slots at the native positions; the result is the first matching unspent offer (preferred index when 1 .. size - 1, either payment order); taking it calls `doTrade` in both orders, `useRecipe` and recalculates; closing drops both payment stacks and releases the customer.
- Villager side effects: `useRecipe` raises uses, sets the living-sound counter to -80, plays `mob.villager.yes`, and when the recipe equals the last offer sets timeUntilReset 40, needsInit and the last buyer; an emerald buy price adds to Riches. Every recalculation plays `mob.villager.yes` (result) or `mob.villager.no` at most once per 20 ticks with pitch from the villager's Random (child +0.5). While nobody trades the timer counts down; at 0 a flagged villager raises max uses of spent offers by `nextInt(6) + nextInt(6) + 2` (only with more than one offer), adds one more offer and always gains Regeneration for 200 ticks.
- Screen: `gui/container/villager.png` background, title centred, "Inventory" label, three offer icons with counts and tooltips, arrow buttons (enabled, hover, disabled sprites, `gui.button.press` sound), the spent-offer crosses, and the same slot, cursor, drag and shift-click behaviour as the other container screens.
- Saving: the browser entity record keeps `offers` (original item ids), `profession` and `riches`; the customer and timers start over on load (`readEntityFromNBT`). Java export writes `Offers.Recipes` (`buy`, optional `buyB`, `sell`, `uses`, `maxUses`) in 1.7.10 layout, in original ids with enchantment tags. Tested in `src/villager-persistence.test.js` and `src/merchant-ui.test.js`.

## Open, approximated or not done

- Village reputation: a restock with a remembered buyer gives the village +1 reputation for "Player" and a happy-villager burst (state 14), as `EntityVillager.updateAITick`; hitting or killing villagers lowers it (see `village.md`). The merchant screen does not apply the reputation price modifier because 1.7.10 has none.
- Offers keep original item ids. Items with no browser counterpart in this build (original 350, 387, 347, 384, 89) are shown as "?" and can neither be paid with nor taken; the trade state still counts such an offer as having a result for the yes sound, as native does.
- Java import does not read villagers at all (`region-import.js` imports dropped items only), so Offers are exported but not re-imported.
- The browser view of spent offers and tooltips comes from `GuiMerchant` bytecode (layout read from `bfv`), not pixel comparison. The crosses are drawn at y 21 and 51 when the selected offer is spent.
- Only the local player can trade; the customer is a flag, not an entity reference. Holding a spawn egg is not detected (spawn eggs are not usable on mobs here). Villager sounds follow the entity event queue, so the yes and no sounds play when the screen refreshes.
- The villager's profession is not changed by trading and professions above 4 get only the gold-ingot fallback offer, as native.
