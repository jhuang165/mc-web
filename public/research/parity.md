# Minecraft Java 1.7.10 feature coverage

A batch-13 track (unreleased) imports a Java save's [level.dat, player and stats](level-dat.md): the world continues streamed with the save's seed, WorldType and Superflat options, game rules, mode, spawn, time and weather, and the player keeps inventory, ender chest, health, food, XP, effects, bed spawn and position; region import now also carries paintings, item frames and every minecart type. 38 native WorldInfo/EntityPlayer records (LevelDatProbe) back it; MapFeatures off, hardcore play, flying and the UUID are approximated or not carried.

Batch 13 [controls](controls.md) (unreleased): every KeyBinding can be rebound on the Controls screen, with mouse buttons, conflict highlighting and Reset/Reset Keys, and all game input reads the bindings as LWJGL codes. It also adds a native-format options.txt mirror, F1 hide-GUI, the 1.7.10 F3 combinations (no F8), and Max Framerate/Use VSync. Anaglyph is still missing.

A batch-13 natural spawning track (unreleased) replaces the night ring and the separate slime, enderman, squid/bat, ocelot and Nether/End passes with one port of SpawnerAnimals.findChunksForSpawning driven by every biome's spawn lists, with the 1.7.10 caps, chunk order, group data, the 400-tick animal cadence, Peaceful/doMobSpawning and the fortress, Hell, End and witch hut lists ([natural spawning](natural-spawning.md)). Three seeded native replays (360 ticks, 361 spawns) match exactly; eligible chunks are the browser's ticking window rather than 289 and other approximations are listed in the doc.

A batch-13 commands track (unreleased) adds [chat components and /tellraw, /achievement, /spreadplayers, /debug and /scoreboard](commands-b13.md). It covers the scoreboard sidebar, the Tab score column, team prefixes in chat, live criteria, per-world saving and `data/scoreboard.dat` export/import. CommandScoreboard output, state and NBT are replayed from 225 original-class probe records (scoreboard-reference.json). Limits: belowName, friendlyfire and seeFriendlyInvisibles have no second player to act on, criteria completion order is approximate, and /debug profiles browser tick phases. No registered command reports itself unavailable now; full parity is not claimed.

A batch-13 render fix (unreleased, [render settings](render-settings.md)): Fancy leaves, tall grass and ferns no longer show the page through as silvery speckles at range. Non-blended world and entity materials write alpha 1, as 1.7.10 draws into an opaque window; the mipmap transparency flag checks only the first `levels + 1` texels, as `TextureUtil.generateMipmapData` (bqi.a(II[[I)) does, so jungle leaves average alpha natively. Checked headless at Mipmap Levels 4 against a magenta backdrop: 22,308 show-through pixels before, 0 after.

A batch-12 set (unreleased) adds [Chat Settings](chat-options.md) with the Tab list and F2 screenshots, Pumpkin Pie, the Enchanted Golden Apple and the remaining monster eggs ([food items](food-items.md)), [Book and Quill and Written Book](books.md) with the book screen, the [Superflat](superflat.md) world type with its screens and the bonus chest, and the [spawner minecart](spawner-cart.md) with wider `/summon` coverage and `Riding` mounts.

A batch-11 set of five tracks (unreleased) makes the Video Settings Graphics, Smooth Lighting, Clouds, Mipmap Levels and Anisotropic Filtering options work ([render settings](render-settings.md)), adds [statistics](stats.md) with the Statistics screen and `stats/<uuid>.json` in the Java export, saves rider chains in Java regions and keeps jockeys out of portals ([mob riding](mob-riding.md)), adds [void fog](void-fog.md) and the summon-only [Giant](giant.md), and refreshes every parity catalogue with a checked [remaining-gaps](remaining-gaps.md) list.

After 0.43.0 (unreleased): every mob now plays its [footsteps](mob-steps.md) from the `Entity.moveEntity` step distance (own events for zombie, skeleton, spiders, pig, cow, sheep, chicken and iron golem; the block SoundType for creeper, villager and witch; swim sounds in water; silverfish silent). 0.41.0 adds potion visuals, zombie villagers and native block sounds. [Potion effects](potion-fx.md) now show on screen: night vision brightens the lightmap and fog, blindness closes the fog to 5 blocks and hides the sky, nausea warps the view through the native portal timer, and invisible players and mobs stop rendering while armour and held items stay; splash potions burst into item and spell particles with the smash sound and are saved in flight (8 native records, ~3,300 cases). Zombies now hunt villagers and convert them on Normal (half the time) and Hard; a golden apple cures a weakened [zombie villager](zombie-villager.md) over 3600-6000 ticks, sped up by iron bars (26 native records). Java imports and exports keep `villages.dat`, villagers, iron golems and zombie villagers. Every block's dig, hit, place and step sound now comes from its native 1.7.10 [sound type](block-sounds.md) (171 blocks), so rails and iron blocks play the metal sound at pitch 1.5. Open: mob AI ignores the invisibility detection range, potions in flight are not written to region files, mob footsteps are not wired. Full parity remains unfinished.
