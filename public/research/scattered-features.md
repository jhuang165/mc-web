# Java 1.7.10 scattered features (GEN-05e)

Status: **partial, standalone**. `src/scattered-feature-generator.js` ports the desert pyramid, jungle pyramid and swamp hut with their start/placement classes. It is checked against the unchanged pinned 1.7.10 classes and is wired into live world generation (finite v14 and streamed v15; `src/structure-world.test.js`, [structure area](structure-area.md)). Its traps run live since batch 15: tripwire hook and string, the jungle lever puzzle and the desert plate-and-TNT trap (see [Live traps](#live-traps-batch-15)). This is not a parity claim for structures; villages, strongholds, nether fortresses and ocean features are separate work.

`node tools/verify-scattered-features.mjs` compiles `tools/probes/ScatteredFeatureProbe.java` against the SHA-1-pinned `minecraft-1.7.10.jar` (inputs in the ignored `tools/research/`; JDK 19.0.1 recorded) and writes `public/research/scattered-feature-reference.json` (about 1.3 MB). `src/scattered-feature-generator.test.js` replays it with `node --test`.

Unchanged native classes executed: MapGenBase/MapGenStructure (`aqx`/`ave`) and its `HashMap<Long, StructureStart>`, MapGenScatteredFeature (`atx`) with its Start (`aty`), the ComponentScatteredFeaturePieces `Feature` base (`aue`), DesertPyramid (`aub`), JunglePyramid (`auc`) with the Stones selector (`aud`), SwampHut (`auf`), StructureComponent/StructureBoundingBox, WeightedRandomChestContent, the level-30 enchanted book, the real `TileEntityChest` and `TileEntityDispenser`, and the real `EntityWitch`. The World is an explicit block-access double (see limits).

Replayed exactly by the test:

Per call: setter x/y/z/id/meta/flags and read-order SHA-1 hashes, with the full write list kept for 40 chunk calls; witch spawns; chest and dispenser contents with every `setInventorySlotContents` call; the piece fields after each call (`hPos` and the once-only placed flags); and the Random state afterwards.
- Direct piece calls: 36 pieces, 396 calls (245,857 writes) with partial, thin and Y-limited boxes and boxes missing ground height, sharing one Random stream across calls. This replays the once-only chest, dispenser, trap and witch flags across calls, including the native behaviour that loot Random is consumed on every call while a chest is not yet placed, even when its position is outside the box.

## Rules ported (1.7.10)

- `Feature` constructor draws `nextInt(4)` for orientation; boxes use width x depth for modes 0/2 and depth x width for modes 1/3.
- `func_74935_a` averages `max(World.func_72825_h(x, z), averageGroundLevel)` over columns of the piece inside the clip box (z outer, x inner), fails if none, and shifts the piece once (`hPos >= 0` short-circuits). The desert pyramid does not call it; its start Y is the fixed 64 and its box is already placed.
- Chests are placed through `generateStructureChestContents` and the jungle trap dispensers through the dispenser overload, with metadata mapped by `getMetadataWithOffset`. Redstone parts (wire, repeaters, levers, tripwire hooks, pistons, pressure plate, TNT) are placed as raw id/metadata only by the generator, as the native generation writes them; the live engine runs them afterwards (below).
- The jungle `Stones` selector (cobblestone or mossy cobblestone, 0.4) and the swamp hut witch spawn at `x+0.5, y, z+0.5` (one witch, once).
- Placement reuses `templeCandidate` from `src/structure-placement.js`; MapGenScatteredFeature keeps salt 14357617, `distance` 32 and temple biome slots 2, 17, 21, 22, 6.

## Limits

- No NBT round trip (`describe()` lists the fields the native piece would save); MapGenStructureData persistence is not modelled.
- The World double (the standalone generator checks) has no native Chunk storage, notifications, lighting, entity ticking, block updates, redstone power simulation or flower pot tile entity (the pot contents are not modelled). Live worlds run the traps through `src/redstone.js` instead.
- Native terrain/surface/cave chunks were not used as input; terrain comes from synthetic fixtures. `World.func_72825_h` is implemented as in 1.7.10 over the fixture, so interaction with real generated terrain is unverified here.
- Start and population records inject a forced biome or start for per-type coverage; natural placement is verified separately with the real biome manager (grid records).
- The `StructureMap` iteration order is JVM dependent (recorded on JDK 19.0.1).
- Framework gaps worked around inside the module (no framework file edited): `getStructureAt`-based `isSwampHut`, the dispenser contents overload, the average-height adjust and the `Stones` selector are implemented in `scattered-feature-generator.js`.

## Live traps (batch 15)

- **Jungle pyramid tripwires.** `BlockTripWireHook` (aog, original 131) and `BlockTripWire` (aof, 132) are ported in `src/redstone.js` and replayed by `src/tripwire.test.js` against `public/research/tripwire-reference.json` (33 scenarios, 1330 ticks since batch 17, recorded by `tools/verify-tripwire.mjs` through the unchanged native classes in `tools/probes/RedstoneProbe.java`: placement order with flags 3 and 2, `onPostBlockPlaced`, entity enter and leave, 10-tick rechecks, hook powering through its anchor block into wire and a lamp, z-axis lines, suspended strings, broken hooks, strings, anchors and floors, a disarmed string, gaps and obstructions, the 41-cell range limit, same-facing hooks, a hook replaced while powered; batch 17: shears/stick/empty-hand harvest, random ticks, projectile and bat entity classes). Sounds (`random.click` 0.6/0.5/0.7, `random.bowhit` with the world-Random pitch), drops (string item 287, hook), pending ticks and the world Random match the native run.
- **Generated state.** The generator keeps writing the pyramid's hooks (facing|4) and strings (4) as raw metadata (callbacks are skipped on purpose, `QUIET_*`); the probe scenario `generated-state-trip` shows that inert state equals the native settled line, so nothing runs until something touches a string.
- **Live wiring.** `LiveRedstone` re-senses idle strings every tick (as it does plates; `Entity.func_145775_I` equivalent). Batch 17 narrows that to the entities that actually reach `func_145775_I` (see Batch 17 below). Placing a hook runs `onPostBlockPlaced` after `onBlockPlacedBy`. Shears break a string through `breakPlayerBlock`, which sets metadata |8 first (no neighbour notify) so the break disarms instead of powering the line (`BlockTripWire.onBlockHarvested`, now probed).
- **End to end** (`src/temple-traps.test.js`, finite v14 seed 267): stepping on either trap line powers its wire and the dispenser fires one arrow; a second line fires the other dispenser; the right lever (the third) extends the sticky pistons while the two decoys do nothing; the generated state is stable for 60 idle ticks. The native `BlockDispenser` tick rate (4) is unchanged; the brief said 6.
- **Desert pyramid.** The stone plate (`BlockPressurePlate`, 20-tick rate, living entities) and `BlockTNT` (primes from `isBlockIndirectlyGettingPowered` in `onBlockAdded` and `onNeighborBlockChange`) were already live and native-verified; the plate strongly powers the block below, the sandstone, which powers the TNT under it (test: plate over sandstone over the 3x3 TNT floor primes the TNT at (10,-13,10); the other eight TNT blocks go with the explosion chain). Desert seed 395's temple lies outside the finite 128-block world, so the end-to-end check hand-builds the generator's column rather than loading a generated pyramid.
- **Not probed (bytecode-read only).** The client-side `setBlockBoundsBasedOnState` (the sensing box uses the constructor bounds 0..0.15625 high; native reuses whatever bounds were set last on the shared block) and running the thrown/arrow entities' own `onUpdate` (their `func_145775_I` call sites are checked with javap, not executed). Fireballs and wither skulls never trip strings. Boats and falling blocks reach `func_145775_I` through `moveEntity` and are live since batch 18 (below).
