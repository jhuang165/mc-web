# Original crafting and smelting parity catalogue

Generated by `node tools/build-recipe-catalogue.mjs` without a JDK; refresh original evidence with `node tools/verify-recipe-catalogue.mjs`. [Roadmap](parity-roadmap.md) · [original registry/matching evidence](recipe-registry-reference.json) · [item catalogue](item-parity-catalogue.md).

The unchanged CraftingManager holds **317 ordered entries**: 258 exact ShapedRecipes, 54 exact ShapelessRecipes and five special handlers. FurnaceRecipes has **22 input/result entries** and a separate **22-entry output/XP map**. All appear below; preserve repeated map entries rather than collapsing native ItemStack object keys by value. C indexes preserve native sorted dispatch order; S/X indexes are deterministic stack-sorted audit keys, not native HashMap traversal order or stable cross-version registry IDs.

The extractor also executes **2756 native manager samples**. The browser replays **1450 mapped samples across 145 fixed recipes**, plus **18 mapped furnace result/XP checks**. `src/recipe-catalogue.test.js` locks those replay counts and results. Matching is Partial: positive layouts with wildcard damage sampled as zero do not prove all metadata/NBT values, arbitrary shapeless permutations, rejection cases, container transactions or acquisition behavior. Unmapped means the sampled input/output lacks a current mapping; review before declaring Missing. Matching raw registry counts is not recipe parity.

### Manager repair shortcut and special semantics

Repair is a CraftingManager branch before the registered list, not a sixth registered special recipe. It remains Partial, with [existing original inventory cases](inventory.md): two single-count damageable items of the same kind, original five-percent bonus and damage rounding, plus NBT/result and negative-case acceptance under C/N. The five registered special handlers need separate semantic families: leather armor dyeing, fireworks/charges/fading, written-book cloning, map cloning and map extension/world-data scaling. Fixed registry entries do not enumerate those combinations. Brewing, furnace fuels, anvil combinations, enchanting, villager trades and non-crafting acquisition are separate inventories and remain open.
